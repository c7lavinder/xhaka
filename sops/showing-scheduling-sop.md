**Last Updated:** February 3, 2026
**Status:** Active

## Purpose
Coordinate property showings between buyers and sellers for deals under contract, without requiring the Acquisition Manager's involvement.

---

## When This Process Starts
- Deal moves to "Under Contract" stage
- Buyer requests showing OR showing needs to be scheduled for interested buyers

---

## Information Needed Before Scheduling

### From the Deal/GHL:
- [ ] Property address
- [ ] Seller name & contact info
- [ ] Seller's availability preferences (if known)
- [ ] Any access instructions (lockbox code, gate code, dogs, etc.)
- [ ] Seller's communication preference (call/text)

### From the Buyer:
- [ ] Buyer name & contact info
- [ ] Requested date/time (or flexibility)
- [ ] Who's attending (just buyer? Contractor? Partner?)
- [ ] How long they need (15 min walkthrough? 1 hour inspection?)

---

## Step-by-Step Process

### 1. Buyer Requests Showing
**Trigger:** Buyer reaches out wanting to see property

**Action:**
- [ ] Confirm property address
- [ ] Get buyer's preferred times (at least 2-3 options)
- [ ] Note who's attending and duration needed

**Script:**
> "Happy to get you in there. What times work for you in the next few days? And will it just be you, or bringing anyone else?"

---

### 2. Contact Seller to Confirm
**Action:**
- [ ] Reach out to seller (text preferred unless they prefer calls)
- [ ] Propose the buyer's requested times
- [ ] Confirm access instructions

**Script (text):**
> "Hi [Seller], this is [Name] with New Again Houses. We have a buyer interested in viewing [Property Address]. Would [Day] at [Time] work for a showing? It'll be about [duration]. Let me know what works!"

**If seller is difficult to reach:**
- Try both call and text
- Give 24hr to respond before following up
- Note in GHL if seller is unresponsive

---

### 3. Confirm with Buyer
**Once seller confirms:**
- [ ] Text/call buyer with confirmed time
- [ ] Send property address and any access instructions
- [ ] Set reminder for yourself to follow up after showing

**Script:**
> "You're confirmed for [Property Address] on [Day] at [Time]. [Access instructions if any]. Let me know how it goes!"

---

### 4. Showing Reminders
**Day before showing:**
- [ ] Text reminder to buyer
- [ ] Text reminder to seller (if they need to be present or prep)

**Buyer reminder:**
> "Reminder: Showing tomorrow at [Time] at [Address]. See you there!"

**Seller reminder:**
> "Hi [Seller], just a reminder we have a showing scheduled for tomorrow at [Time]. Thanks!"

---

### 5. Post-Showing Follow-Up
**Within 2-4 hours after scheduled showing:**
- [ ] Text buyer: "How'd the showing go? Any questions?"
- [ ] Note feedback in GHL on the deal/buyer record
- [ ] If buyer wants to make offer or has concerns, loop in appropriate person

---

## Common Scenarios & How to Handle

### Seller wants to reschedule
- Get new availability from seller
- Reach out to buyer with new options
- Apologize for inconvenience, keep it professional

### Buyer no-shows
- Text buyer to confirm they're still coming (if late)
- Apologize to seller if they were waiting
- Note in GHL, follow up with buyer next day

### Seller is unresponsive
- Try call + text
- If no response in 24-48 hrs, escalate to [Kyle? TC?]
- Don't promise buyer a time until seller confirms

### Multiple buyers want same time slot
- Schedule back-to-back if possible (30 min apart)
- Or offer different days
- Don't double-book same time

### Buyer wants to bring contractor/inspector
- Confirm with seller this is okay
- May need longer time slot
- Make sure seller knows who's coming

## Escalation
**When to loop in Kyle:**
- Seller is upset or threatening to back out
- Buyer makes verbal offer during showing
- Major issue discovered at property
- Anything that feels above your pay grade

**When to loop in TC (EZ REI):**
- Questions about closing timeline
- Title/contract issues come up
