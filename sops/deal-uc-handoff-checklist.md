# Deal Under Contract - Handoff Checklist

**Purpose:** When a deal moves to UC, capture everything needed so the AM doesn't get pulled back in for showings or seller questions.

**Current State:** AM who locked up the deal handles showings
**Future State:** VA → eventually AI-automated

---

## Immediate Actions (Within 1 hour of UC)

### 1. Update GHL Deal Record
- [ ] Deal stage → "Under Contract"
- [ ] Contract price entered
- [ ] Closing date entered
- [ ] TC assigned (EZ REI Closings)

### 2. Capture Seller Info for Showings
- [ ] **Seller preferred contact method:** Call / Text / Either
- [ ] **Best times to reach seller:** _______________
- [ ] **Seller availability for showings:** _______________
- [ ] **Anyone else lives there?** _______________
- [ ] **Pets?** Yes / No - Details: _______________
- [ ] **Access instructions:** _______________
  - Lockbox code: _______________
  - Gate code: _______________
  - Key location: _______________
  - Other: _______________
- [ ] **Seller's concerns/sensitivities:** _______________
  *(e.g., "Don't mention the price to neighbors" or "Call before 8pm")*

### 3. Warm Handoff Call/Text to Seller
Kyle (or AM) sends intro:

> "Hey [Seller], excited to move forward! From here, [Name] will be coordinating any showings with buyers. They'll reach out to schedule. I'm still here if you need anything, but [Name] has you covered on the day-to-day. Talk soon!"

- [ ] Handoff message sent
- [ ] Seller acknowledged

### 4. Showing Setup in GHL
- [ ] Create showing appointment in GHL (uses Showing workflow — sends auto reminders)
- [ ] Confirm all seller/access info is in the deal notes
- [ ] If handing off to someone else, share any seller quirks

## Red Flags → Escalate to Kyle
- Seller expressing doubt or wanting to back out
- Major property issue discovered
- Buyer making lowball re-trade attempt
- Seller unreachable for 48+ hours
- Any legal/contract concerns
