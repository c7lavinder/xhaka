# Manus Brief: "Ensuring a Deal a Month" Skool Course

## Overview

Build a complete Skool community and course that teaches real estate wholesalers how to enter any market and consistently close at least one deal per month. This is not theory — it's a proven system based on how New Again Houses Nashville operates in Tennessee.

---

## Positioning & Promise

**Core Promise:** "Enter any market and close a deal every month — consistently, predictably, systematically."

**What makes this different:**
- Not about getting lucky with one deal
- Not about "hustle harder" motivation fluff
- It's a repeatable SYSTEM — the same one used to run a real wholesaling operation in Tennessee
- Designed to work whether you're solo or building a team

**Tagline options:**
- "The system behind consistent deal flow"
- "Stop chasing deals. Start expecting them."
- "From random wins to monthly income"

---

## Target Audience

**Primary:** 
1. **New team members being onboarded** — employees joining an existing wholesaling operation who need to learn the system fast
2. **Wholesalers with a few deals under their belt** — they've proven it works but can't get consistent; tired of feast-or-famine months

**Secondary:**
- Flippers who want to add wholesale as a deal source
- Agents looking to transition into investing
- People in adjacent markets wanting to expand geographically

**NOT for:**
- Complete beginners who've never talked to a seller (though they could follow along)
- People looking for get-rich-quick schemes

**Student mindset:** "I know this works. I just need a system I can follow and eventually hand off to someone else."

---

## Pricing Model

- **$99/month** for external members
- **Free** for internal employees (New Again Houses team)

## Course Structure: 10 Modules

### Module 1: Market Selection
*How to pick where to operate*

**Lessons:**
1. What makes a good wholesale market (population, price points, investor activity)
2. Research tools and data sources (Zillow, Redfin, PropStream, county records)
3. Evaluating competition — is saturation real or a myth?
4. Case study: Why Tennessee / Nashville works
5. Picking your first (or next) market — decision framework
6. Action step: Select and commit to your market

---

### Module 2: Lead Generation
*Getting deal flow in a new market*

**Lessons:**
1. The lead generation menu — all your options (direct mail, cold calling, PPC, SEO, driving for dollars, referrals)
2. Cost per lead vs. cost per deal — what actually matters
3. Starting lean: Best ROI channels when cash is tight
4. Building your marketing calendar
5. Tracking and measuring: Know your numbers from day one
6. Scaling up: When and how to add more channels
7. Case study: NAH's lead gen stack and numbers
8. Action step: Launch your first lead source

---

### Module 3: Lead Management
*Systems to handle volume without dropping balls*

**Lessons:**
1. Why leads die — the follow-up failure most wholesalers make
2. CRM setup: GoHighLevel walkthrough (or alternative CRMs)
3. Pipeline stages: From new lead to closed deal
4. Speed to lead — why response time is everything
5. Automated follow-up sequences that actually work
6. Tagging, segmentation, and organization
7. Daily/weekly lead management routines
8. Action step: Set up your CRM and first automation

---

### Module 4: Talking to Sellers
*Scripts, objections, building rapport*

**Lessons:**
1. Mindset: You're a problem solver, not a salesperson
2. The first call — what to say in the first 60 seconds
3. Building rapport: Getting sellers to actually talk to you
4. Discovery questions: Understanding their real situation
5. Motivation levels: How to qualify (or disqualify) fast
6. Handling objections: "I need to think about it," "Your offer is too low," "I'm talking to other buyers"
7. The follow-up call: Re-engaging cold leads
8. Role play examples and scripts
9. Action step: Practice the script with a partner

---

### Module 5: Making Offers & Negotiation
*How to get to yes*

**Lessons:**
1. When to make an offer (don't jump the gun)
2. The offer formula: ARV, repairs, your fee, buyer's profit
3. Presenting the offer: Anchoring and framing
4. Negotiation fundamentals: Never negotiate against yourself
5. The "walk away" — your most powerful tool
6. Creative deal structures: Subject-to, seller financing, lease options
7. Getting the verbal yes
8. Action step: Make 5 offers this week

---

### Module 6: Valuations
*Knowing your numbers in an unfamiliar market*

**Lessons:**
1. ARV (After Repair Value): What it is and why it's everything
2. Finding comps: MLS access, Zillow, Redfin, PropStream
3. Adjusting comps: Size, condition, location, time
4. Estimating repairs: The quick method vs. detailed SOW
5. Building your repair cost cheat sheet
6. The 70% rule and when to break it
7. Verifying with local buyers (the sanity check)
8. Case study: Walking through a real deal valuation
9. Action step: Valuate 3 properties and verify with a buyer

---

### Module 7: Finding Buyers
*Building a buyer list fast*

**Lessons:**
1. Why your buyer list is your real asset
2. Where to find cash buyers (courthouse, auctions, FB groups, networking)
3. The buyer intake process: What info to collect
4. Qualifying buyers: Proof of funds, speed, reliability
5. Building relationships: Not all buyers are equal
6. Buyer management in your CRM
7. The "I have a deal" blast: How to market to your list
8. Disposition channels: Facebook, Craigslist, investor meetups
9. Action step: Add 20 buyers to your list this week

---

### Module 8: Closing the Deal
*Contracts, coordination, getting paid*

**Lessons:**
1. The assignment contract: What it is and how it works
2. Purchase agreement essentials: Protect yourself
3. Earnest money: How much, when, and where
4. Title company coordination: Your closing partner
5. Double closes vs. assignments: When to use each
6. The closing timeline: Managing all parties
7. Wiring and getting paid: The finish line
8. Celebrating and documenting your win
9. Action step: Get your contracts reviewed and ready

---

### Module 9: KPIs & Metrics
*The numbers that tell you if you're on track*

**Lessons:**
1. What gets measured gets managed
2. The core KPIs: Leads, contacts, appointments, offers, contracts, closes
3. Conversion rates at each stage — benchmarks to aim for
4. Cost per lead, cost per deal, profit per deal
5. Time-based metrics: Days to close, speed to lead
6. Building your KPI dashboard (spreadsheet or CRM)
7. Weekly reviews: How to diagnose problems fast
8. Action step: Set up tracking and run your first weekly review

---

### Module 10: Scaling
*From 1 deal/month to 2, 3, 4...*

**Lessons:**
1. When to scale (don't scale chaos)
2. The first hire: Acquisitions, dispositions, or admin?
3. Documenting your processes (SOPs)
4. Training and onboarding team members
5. Compensation structures that align incentives
6. Quality control: Maintaining deal quality as you grow
7. Adding markets: Replicating in a new geography
8. The owner's role: From doer to operator
9. Action step: Write your first SOP

---

## Tone & Style Guidelines

**Voice:**
- Direct and practical — no fluff
- Confident but not arrogant
- Like a mentor who's done it, not a guru selling dreams
- Conversational, not academic

**Examples:**
- ✅ "Here's exactly what to say when a seller ghosts you"
- ✅ "This is going to feel uncomfortable. Do it anyway."
- ❌ "Unlock your potential and manifest abundance"
- ❌ "In this module, we will explore the theoretical frameworks..."

**Format:**
- Short paragraphs, lots of white space
- Bullet points for lists
- Real examples from Tennessee deals where possible
- Each lesson ends with a clear action step
