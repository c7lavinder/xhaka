## Course Overview

```
NAH Operations Playbook (aligned to Process Map phases)
├── 1. Company Overview .............. Context & big picture
├── 2. Lead Generation ............... Phase 1 (Data Manager + Cold Callers)
├── 3. Lead Management (LM) .......... Phases 2, 3, 4, 8 (Research → Apt + Follow Up)
├── 4. Acquisitions (AM) ............. Phases 5, 6, 7 (Walkthrough → Contract)
├── 5. Disposition ................... Post-contract (Buyer side)
├── 6. Operations & Admin ............ Supporting functions
├── 7. Tools & Systems ............... GHL, MasterSuite, Gunner
└── 8. Templates & Resources ......... Ready-to-use materials
```

| Phase | Name | Owner | Module |
|-------|------|-------|--------|
| 1 | Lead Generation | Data + Callers | Module 2 |
