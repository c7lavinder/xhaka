# DISPO LEAD GEN Process Map
*Extracted from Corey's Lucidchart — Feb 15, 2026*

## Core Principle
> Put deals in front of as many people as possible in the first 48 hours. If it's a good deal, it will sell.

---

## Page 1: Process Flow

### Main Pipeline (left → right)
1. **Expectations w/ Seller Set** — align on timeline, price, terms
2. **High Quality Pictures** — professional photos of property
3. **Property Details and numbers** — ARV, repair estimate, contract price, etc.
4. **Property Website Created** — dedicated listing page for the deal
5. **Start Marketing** *(decision point)* — green light to push deal out
6. → Splits into **Buyer Hunts** and **Buyer Lists**

---

### Branch 1: Buyer Hunts (active outbound)
Finding NEW buyers through research and outreach:

| Channel | Actions |
|---------|---------|
| **InvestorLift** | Create Property Page → Create marketing campaigns → Add Buyer to DNC/list |
| **Mevlo** | Download Realtor/Redfin numbers → Create Messages CSV |
| **Zillow Rentals** | Look up property → Filters → Filter rentals on Zillow → See if listed by owner |
| **MLS/Listed Flips** | Filter listed/sold on Zillow → Find contact of agent |
| **Facebook Groups** | Join FB Groups → Look for wholesaler posts |
| **Mass Campaigns** *(yellow node)* | Bulk outreach across channels |

### Branch 2: Buyer Lists (existing database)
Leveraging known buyer databases:

| Platform | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **Privy** | Navigate to active buyers in market |
| **OpenDeal** | Navigate to qualified buyers in market |
| **JV (Joint Ventures)** | Navigate to JVs in area |

## Page 3: Dispo Team Roles

### Owner (Corey)
*"The engine to process. Managing growth by delegating tasks, providing energy throughout the business, and ensuring an increase in revenue."*
- Final Decision Maker
- Motivates Dispo team to perform
- Ensures lead gen engine is firing
- Ensures everyone is paid on time
- Monitors the books

### Dispo Admin
*"Present through entire life of opportunity by ensuring TC is progressing escrow and Dispo Mgnr is hitting KPIs"*
- Creates marketing campaigns
- Sends out contracts
- Sends buyer/seller details to TC
- Monitors TC progress
- Ensures Dispo Mgnr KPIs are hit
- Tracks KPIs accurately

### TC (Transaction Coordinator)
*"Takes over once deal under contract. Maintains conversations with buyer and seller to ensure smooth transaction."*
- Opens escrow with title
- Sends out any amendments
- Updates buyer/seller quickly
- Handles communication for title issues
- Maintains communication with seller/buyer
- Ensures buyer/seller perform on closing date

### Dispo Mgnr (Esteban)
*"Takes over once property is ready to be sent out. Responsible for finding highest best fit buyer and ensuring they complete the closing."*
- Executes marketing campaigns
- Creates opportunities for buyers
- Qualifies buyers and organizes them in CRM
- Schedules showings
- Generates offers from buyers
- Ensures executed agreements

## Weekly Dispo Schedule (confirmed Feb 15)

| Day | Focus | Details |
|-----|-------|---------|
| **Mon-Tue** | Exclusive buyer list push | New deals go to existing buyer database first |
| **Wed** | Public blast | Deals published everywhere (InvestorLift, Mevlo, Facebook, etc.) |
| **Thu-Fri** | Inbound + Buyer Hunts | Respond to buyer leads, active outbound research |

### Express Lane (exception, not habit)
Deals bypass the schedule ONLY when:
- Seller has short due diligence / urgent timeline
- High competition risk (PPL lead, multiple wholesalers on it)
- Margin justifies immediate push

Express lane = Corey approves, bot blasts immediately. Default is always the schedule.

### Deal Packaging Deliverables
When a deal is fully packaged, bot auto-generates:
1. **Professional PDF** — photos, numbers (Ask/ARV/Margin), property details, comps, offer terms, contact info
2. **Email template** — subject line + body with key numbers + PDF attached
3. Both attached to GHL opportunity, ready for distribution

## Automation Opportunities
The bot can automate:
1. **Deal packaging** → property website + details + photos (already built in engine)
2. **Multi-platform posting** → auto-push to InvestorLift, Mevlo, FB when deal is ready
3. **Buyer hunt automation** → pull Zillow rentals, MLS flips, Redfin numbers
4. **Buyer list queries** → Privy/OpenDeal/JV lookups
5. **Response tracking** → log buyer interest back to GHL
6. **Mass campaign execution** → blast to buyer database
7. **48-hour clock** → track time since deal posted, escalate if no offers
