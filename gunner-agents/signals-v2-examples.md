# Signals V2 — Real Examples

## Example 1: "Stand By" Instead of Booking Appointment

**Lead:** Robin Phelps
**Agent:** Daniel Lozano (LM)
**Date:** Feb 12, 2026

**What happened:**
- Seller is out of state, said she'll be in town first part of March
- Mother is bed-bound, on hospice — property can't sell until she passes or goes to assisted living
- Seller said "I don't know if that's weeks or months" — clear life event with uncertain but real timeline
- Daniel responded with empathy but ended with "I can be on standby... feel free to reach out anytime"
- No appointment set, no follow-up date, no calendar commitment

**What should have happened:**
- Book the March appointment immediately — seller offered the opening ("I'll be there first part of March")
- "Let's go ahead and set something up for when you're in town early March — even if the timeline shifts, it gives us a chance to meet and walk through everything together"
- Set a follow-up reminder for late February to confirm the March meeting
- This is a HIGH motivation lead — hospice situation means it could accelerate at any time

**Detection signals:**
- Seller mentioned life event (hospice, mother's health)
- Seller gave a timeline ("weeks or months," "first part of March")
- Seller expressed willingness to meet
- Agent response contained no next step / no appointment / no follow-up date
- Conversation ended open-ended ("reach out anytime")

**Why this matters:**
Being the trusted buyer already in the picture when the moment comes is the whole game. Empathy + goodbye loses deals. Empathy + next step wins them.
