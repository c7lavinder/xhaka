## 3. Screen Recording Instructions

**Goal:** Create a 90-second video that feels like a high-end software trailer.

**Tools:**
- **Recording:** Loom (clean mode, no camera bubble) or OBS Studio (for higher quality/framerate).
- **Resolution:** 1080p or 4k. Aspect ratio 16:9.
- **Browser:** Chrome (Hide bookmarks bar, hide extensions). Use Full Screen (F11) for the dashboard.

**Step-by-Step Recording Flow:**

1.  **Preparation:**
    - Open `demo-dashboard.html` in your browser.
    - Set Zoom to 100% or 110% so text is legible.
    - Ensure your mouse cursor is visible but not distracting (default size is fine).

2.  **The Take (Match this to the script timing):**
    - **(Start)** Static shot of the full dashboard. Wait 2 seconds.
    - **(Action)** Move mouse smoothly to the "Revenue Saved" card. Hover for 2 seconds so the tooltip/hover state triggers (if added).
    - **(Action)** Move mouse to the Leaderboard row for "Chris P." Click or hover to simulate selecting him.
    - **(Action)** Scroll down smoothly to the "Call Detail" section.
    - **(Action)** Hover over the "1:22" timeline item. Pause there. Let the viewer read "Missed pricing objection".
    - **(Action)** Move mouse right to the "AI Coaching" box. Slowly highlight the suggested text ("I understand — most of our customers..."). This draws the eye.
    - **(Action)** Move mouse down to "Follow-up Actions". Point at "Auto-sent".
    - **(Action)** Scroll back up to the top. Click the "Trend" or "Revenue Impact" header on the table to simulate sorting (even if it's static, just clicking the header implies interaction).
    - **(End)** Leave mouse still on the Logo for 2 seconds.

3.  **Post-Production:**
    - Speed up the scrolling parts if they look slow.
    - Add the "Hook" text overlays in your video editor (CapCut, Premiere, Descript).
    - Record the voiceover separately and layer it on top.
    - Add subtle background music (driving, tech-focused beat, low volume).
