# Lead Generator (Cold Caller) Script

**Purpose:** First contact with property owners. Gauge interest, qualify, and set appointments for Lead Managers or Acquisition Managers.

---

## 1. Intro

> "Hey [Seller Name], *(pause)* it's [Name]. I've been trying to reach the owner of [123 Main St]. You're probably not the owner?"

**If they say "Yes, I am the owner":**
> "Okay great! I'm calling because we're looking to buy a property in your area. You probably wouldn't be interested in selling your property on [Street Name]?"

- **If interested in selling** → Go to Setting Expectations
- **If not interested** → See below

**If they say "No, I'm not interested in selling":**
> "Would there be a time in the future where you'd even consider selling your property?"

**If still no:**
> "I totally understand that. Just so you know, we give a **$2,000 referral** to anyone who sends us a property that we end up buying. I assume you're probably not interested in that?"

---

## 2. Setting Expectations

> "That's great. I'm working with a private investor who's looking to buy properties out of [City]. They do buy them for **all cash, as-is, and at no closing cost**."
>
> "I just need to ask a few questions about your property and situation to find out if we're a good fit to work together or not. Sound fair?"

---

## 3. Confirm Property Condition

- Confirm beds/baths
- *"Have there been any recent updates done or big issues that I should know about?"*
- *"Are you currently living in the property, is it tenant-occupied, or vacant?"*
- *"Are there any repairs needed to the property?"*

**If tenant-occupied:**
- *"Is there a lease or month-to-month?"*
- *"When does the lease expire?"*
- *"Could you give tenants a 60-day notice?"*

---

## 4. Timeline

> "If we agreed on price today, how soon were you looking to start the process of selling your home?"
>
> *(Examples: less than 30 days, 2-3 months, or 60+ days)*

---

## 5. Confirm Roadblocks

**Check LM notes first, then confirm:**

> "Is there anyone who has input on the decision to sell the property?"

> "Okay, but does anybody have veto power to stop you from selling?"

---

## 6. Motivation

> "[Seller name], I know we've been on the phone for a couple minutes now — what even had you looking to sell the property?"

**If no motivation:**
> "Okay, so other than us calling, you probably don't have a reason to sell?"
- If NO → Go to Price

### Diving Deeper Into Motivation

Use mirroring and labeling:
- *"It seems like..."*
- *"It feels like..."*
- *"It sounds like..."*

Probing questions:
- *"How long has this been going on?"*
- *"Can you tell me more about that?"*
- *"What have you tried to do about it?"*
- *"When you say [repeat pain point], what do you mean?"*
- *"How long have you been thinking about this?"*
- *"How long have you been dealing with all of this?"*
- *"How's that make you feel?"*
- *"If you do decide to sell, where do you think you might go? Why there?"*

---

## 7. Price

> "Okay, how much were you looking to get for the property?"

**If they say "I don't know" (Attempt 1):**
> "Gotcha... so you probably don't have a range you'd want to get?"

**If they say "No" again (Attempt 2):**
> "Gotcha, in order to have the best chance of working together, I'm assuming not even like a 10-20k price range? That way we can assist you more effectively."

---

### Price Anchoring

> "So I just want to let you know — I see other investors, and keep in mind this is by no means our offer, but other investors are buying properties at **40-50% of Zillow/comparable value**. Again, that's not our offer, just what other investors are buying properties for cash at. What would you say if they offered you something like that?"

**If they say "WAY too low" or "Hell no":**
> "Again, like I said, that's not our offer — that's just what other investors are buying properties at. What were you hoping I was going to at least say?"

**If they say "Yes, that would work":**
> "Okay [seller name], I do think we could be a great fit to work together. With that being said, if you have an extra 5-10 minutes, I'd love to get you transferred over to my home buying specialist who can formally present you with that offer. Is that okay?"

---

### If Price Is Given

> "Thank you for that. Is that the best you can do? *(PAUSE)* I want to make sure, so when I present the number to my finance department, it will have the best chance of being accepted."

**If YES** → Go to Set Expectations for Next Call

**If NO:**
> "Gotcha, what were you thinking?"

*(If they drop price and have motivation → Go to "If we are a fit")*

---

## 8. Set Expectations for Next Call

### IF NOT A FIT (Disqualify)

> "Okay, I believe I have all the information I need."
>
> "Right now [Seller First Name], I don't think we're the best fit. I think your best option to get the highest price would be to list it with a realtor or hold onto the property."
>
> "We'll follow up with you in the next 30-60 days just to check in. If something ever changes, you have my phone number — you can call or text me anytime. Save my number so you recognize it in the future."
>
> "Thank you."

---

### IF WE ARE A FIT (Give an Offer)

> "Okay, I believe I have all the information I need right now. With that being said, I do believe we'd be a great fit to work together."
>
> "I would love to get you an offer for your property. I just need to send this to finance, which takes about 30 minutes. Are you available then for a conversation?"

**If not available in 30 mins:**
> "Okay, are you available at [TIME 1] or [TIME 2]?"

> "All I ask from you is that you make a confident yes or no decision — and NO is perfectly okay. Also, if there are any other individuals that have input on the sale of the property, they should be on the call too. If everything looks good and you like our offer, would you be in the position to go over an agreement with me then?"

**If they ask for more info or website:**
> "If you'd like, I can share our website with you where you can see some testimonial reviews on us. Is that okay? **[website.com]**"

**Confirming appointment:**
> "Great, will you be available at [TIME 1] or [TIME 2] for [Acquisition Manager Name] to give you a call to present you with that offer?"

> "Perfect, and during the call, if you liked our offer, what would happen next?"

- If YES → Close out the call
- If NOT → *"I understand, when is a better time that will work for you to be able to do that?"* → Schedule at new time

---

## Common Objections

**"I have a tenant in place."**
> "Okay, so what's the tenant's situation — month-to-month or on a set lease?"
> "You must be telling me that for a reason..."
*(Transition into Motivation)*

**"I don't need to sell."**
> "Should we even continue talking? We're not always a good fit."

**"I can list it on the market and it will sell today."**
> "Why have you not listed it on the market?"

**SMS Lead asking at/above market value:**
> Go into Motivation ASAP

---

## Red/Hostile Seller

**"How'd you get my info?"**
> "I don't know how we got your number. My manager just gives me a list of numbers to call. Do you want to sell your property?"

**"Why do you keep calling me?"**
> "We want to see if you're looking to sell your house. You probably don't want to?"

**"I'm currently fixing it up myself."**
> "Sounds like everything has been going good with the remodel. You probably don't want to sell to someone like us then?"
