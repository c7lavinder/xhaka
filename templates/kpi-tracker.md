## How to Use

1. **Update weekly** — Every Monday, fill in last week's numbers
2. **Review conversion rates** — Where are you losing deals?
3. **Compare to benchmarks** — Are you above or below?
4. **Identify bottlenecks** — Low contact rate? Work on speed to lead. Low offer-to-contract? Work on negotiation.
5. **Track cost efficiency** — If CPD is too high, your marketing or conversion needs work

---

## Notes

- **Leads** = New seller leads that came in (inbound + outbound)
- **Contacts** = Leads you actually had a conversation with
- **Appts** = Offer calls or walkthroughs scheduled
- **Offers** = Verbal or written offers made
- **Contracts** = Signed purchase agreements
- **Closed** = Deals that closed and you got paid
