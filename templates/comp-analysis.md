## Comp Criteria

**Good comps are:**
- Sold within last 6 months (3 months better)
- Within 1 mile (0.5 miles better)
- Similar size (within 20% sq ft)
- Similar bed/bath count
- Similar style
- Similar condition AFTER repair

**Minimum 3 comps. 5 is better.**

## Adjustment Guidelines

| Factor | Typical Adjustment |
|--------|-------------------|
| Sq Ft (per sq ft) | $50 - $100 |
| Bedroom (each) | $5,000 - $10,000 |
| Bathroom (each) | $5,000 - $7,500 |
| Garage (yes/no) | $5,000 - $15,000 |
| Pool (yes/no) | $10,000 - $20,000 |
| Age (per 10 years) | $2,000 - $5,000 |
| Lot size (per 0.1 acre) | $1,000 - $5,000 |

**Note:** Adjustments vary by market. Learn your local adjustments over time.

## Notes

- Compare to properties in **AFTER-REPAIR** condition
- If subject needs work, comps should be renovated/updated properties
- When in doubt, be conservative (lower ARV = safer offers)
- Always verify with a local buyer before committing to a contract
