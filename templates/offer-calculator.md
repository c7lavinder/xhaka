## MAO Calculation

### Standard Formula (70% Rule)

```
MAO = (ARV × 70%) - Repairs - Your Fee
```

| Component | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| ARV | $ |
| × 70% | $ |
| - Repairs | $ |
| - Your Fee | $ |
| **= MAO** | **$** |

### Adjustable Formula

| Market Condition | Multiply ARV By |
|------------------|-----------------|
| Hot market (buyers pay more) | 75-80% |
| Normal market | 70% |
| Cold market (more risk) | 65% |
| Heavy rehab (more risk) | 65% or less |
