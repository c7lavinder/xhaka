## Summary
6-month roadmap from zero to AI engineer. Month 1: Python/Git/APIs. Month 2: LLM fundamentals. Month 3: RAG. Month 4: Agents. Month 5: Deployment. Month 6: Specialize (AI product engineer / Applied ML / AI automation).

## Key Insights
- Each month: learn the topic, immediately apply in real work
- 3 specialization paths by month 6: product engineer, applied ML, or AI automation (workflow/CRM/ops)
- Agents = tool selection, state management, retries, evals — not just prompting
