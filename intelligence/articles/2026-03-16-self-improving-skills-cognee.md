## Summary
Skills degrade silently. The self-improvement loop: Ingest → Observe → Inspect → Amend → Evaluate. Without Evaluate, self-improvement is uncontrolled modification.

## Key Insights
- Every amendment must prove it worked or get reverted
- Observe = log what ran, success/fail, error, user feedback
- Inspect = trace recurring factors behind bad outcomes
- Amend = targeted change grounded in failure evidence

## Xhaka Gap Analysis
- Ingest ✅, Observe ✅ (job-registry), Amend 🟡 (proposed-changes)
- Missing: Inspect (linking failures to specific instructions) + Evaluate (did amendment improve?)
