# How Call Scoring Works

Every call you make gets automatically graded by Gunner's AI. Here's how to read your scores and use them to get better.

---

## What Each Grade Means

| Grade | Translation |
|-------|------------|
| **A (90-100%)** | Crushed it — hit every mark, textbook execution |
| **B (80-89%)** | Solid call — did most things right, minor tweaks needed |
| **C (70-79%)** | Room to improve — got the basics but missed opportunities |
| **D (60-69%)** | Missed key steps — review the coaching tips closely |
| **F (Below 60%)** | Needs a rewatch — significant parts of the framework were skipped |

---

## What You're Scored On

### Lead Managers (Daniel, Chris)
1. **Opening & Rapport** — Did you build connection early?
2. **Qualification Questions** — Did you ask the right questions to understand the situation?
3. **Motivation Discovery** — Did you uncover *why* they want to sell?
4. **Objection Handling** — How did you handle pushback?
5. **Close / Next Steps** — Did you set a clear next step or appointment?

### Acquisition Manager (Kyle)
1. **Appointment Confirmation & Setup** — Did you control the frame from the start?
2. **Property & Situation Deep-Dive** — Did you fully understand the deal?
3. **Motivation & Timeline** — Did you nail down urgency and reason?
4. **Offer Presentation** — How did you present numbers and handle reactions?
5. **Commitment & Next Steps** — Did you get a clear yes or defined follow-up?

### Dispo (Esteban)
1. **Buyer Engagement** — Did you connect and build interest?
2. **Property Presentation** — Did you sell the deal clearly?
3. **Buyer Qualification** — Did you confirm they can actually close?
4. **Objection Handling** — How did you address concerns?
5. **Close / Next Steps** — Did you lock down the next action?

---

## About the Scores Right Now

**64% of all graded calls are F's right now.** Before you stress — that's normal.

The rubric is based on **perfect execution** of every step. Nobody walks in and scores an A on day one. The grading is strict on purpose — it catches things you didn't even realize you were missing.

**What matters is the trend, not the letter.** If you're at 35% this week and 50% next week, you're winning. The guys who improve fastest are the ones who actually read their coaching tips after each call.

---

## How to Improve

1. **After each call**, go to Gunner and check your score
2. **Read the coaching tips** — they tell you exactly what you missed and what to say next time
3. **Focus on one thing per day** — don't try to fix everything at once
4. **Watch your weekly average** — that's the number that matters

The coaching tips are specific to YOUR calls. They're not generic advice. Use them.
