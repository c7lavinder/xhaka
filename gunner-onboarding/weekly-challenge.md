# 🏆 Gunner Weekly Challenge

## How It Works

Every week, Corey drops a challenge in the group chat. Hit the goal, win the prize. Simple.

---

## Challenge Templates

### 🎯 Highest Single Call Score
*"This week's challenge: Who can get the highest score on a single call?"*
- Winner = highest individual call score by Friday EOD
- Tiebreaker: whoever hit it first

### 📈 Most Improved
*"This week's challenge: Who improves the most from last week's average?"*
- Compare this week's average to last week's
- Biggest jump wins — doesn't matter where you started

### 🔥 Streak Challenge
*"This week's challenge: Who can string together the most calls above 50%?"*
- Consecutive calls scoring 50%+ or higher
- Longest streak by Friday wins

### 💪 No F's Challenge
*"This week's challenge: Go the whole week without an F."*
- If multiple people do it, highest average wins
- If nobody does it, fewest F's wins

### 🏅 Perfect Category
*"This week's challenge: Get a 100% on any single scoring category in a call."*
- First person to nail a perfect score in any of the 5 categories wins

## How to Run It

1. **Monday AM** — Drop the challenge in the group chat
2. **Mid-week** — Post a leaderboard update to keep energy up
3. **Friday PM** — Announce the winner, give the prize, start trash talk for next week

Keep it fun. The goal is to make people *want* to check their scores.
