# Gunner Quick-Start Guide

**Gunner is an AI coach that listens to your calls and shows you exactly how to close more deals.**

---

## How to Log In

1. Go to **[getgunner.ai](https://getgunner.ai)**
2. Click **Sign in with Google**
3. Use the Google account Corey set up for you
4. You're in

---

## What You'll See

Your calls are **automatically pulled in and graded**. No extra steps — just make your calls like normal.

For each call, Gunner gives you:
- **A letter grade** (A through F)
- **Coaching tips** — specific things you did well and what to tighten up
- **XP and badges** — you earn points for improving and hitting milestones

---

## How Scores Work

Grades are based on how closely a call follows the proven framework. Here's what they mean:

| Grade | What It Means |
|-------|--------------|
| **A** | Crushed it — textbook call |
| **B** | Solid execution, minor tweaks |
| **C** | Good effort, room to improve |
| **D** | Missed some key steps |
| **F** | Needs review — the rubric caught gaps |

**Real talk:** Most calls right now are scoring low. That's normal. The rubric is grading against *perfect* execution — nobody starts at an A. What matters is your score going **up** week over week.

---

## The Leaderboard

Gunner tracks everyone's scores on a leaderboard. You'll see where you stack up against the team. Earn XP for:
- High call scores
- Improving from your previous scores
- Streaks and consistency

Badges unlock as you hit milestones. It's friendly competition — use it.

---

## Your Goal

**Get your score up week over week.** That's it.

After every call, check your coaching tips. They tell you exactly what you missed and how to fix it. Follow the tips, watch your scores climb, close more deals.

The guys who use the coaching tips improve fast. Don't ignore them.
