# Gunner Admin Guide — For Corey

Quick reference for managing the team through Gunner.

---

## Daily Check (2 minutes)

### Control Room
The Control Room is your live dashboard. Check it daily for:
- **Recent calls** and their grades — spot who's struggling and who's improving
- **Flagged calls** — anything that needs your attention
- **Activity feed** — who's actually logging in and reviewing their feedback

### Pipeline Signals
Pipeline Signals flag patterns in calls that indicate deal movement:
- **Hot leads** — calls where motivation and urgency were confirmed
- **At risk** — leads where objections weren't handled or next steps weren't set
- **Stalled** — leads going cold based on call patterns

Use these to prioritize your pipeline review. If a lead shows "at risk," pull up that call and see what happened.

---

## Weekly Review (15 minutes)

### Analytics Page
Go to Analytics to see:
- **Team averages** — overall and by person
- **Trend lines** — who's improving, who's flat, who's dropping
- **Score distribution** — how many A's, B's, C's, D's, F's across the team
- **Category breakdowns** — which scoring categories are weakest across the team

**What to look for:**
- If the whole team is weak in one category (e.g., "Objection Handling"), that's a training opportunity
- If one person is dropping while others improve, that's a 1-on-1 conversation
- Week-over-week trends matter more than individual scores

## 1-on-1 Coaching Sessions

This is where Gunner pays for itself.

### Before the Meeting
1. Pull up their **lowest-scored call** from the past week
2. Pull up their **highest-scored call** from the past week
3. Note the coaching tips on each

### During the Meeting
1. **Start with the win** — play their best call, show them what they did right
2. **Then the growth area** — play the low call, walk through what Gunner flagged
3. **Compare the two** — "Hear the difference? On this call you asked about their timeline. On this one you skipped it. That's the gap."
4. **Pick ONE thing** to focus on this week — not five, just one
5. **Set the target** — "Let's get your average above 50% this week" (or whatever makes sense)

### Pro Tips
- Don't make it about the grade — make it about the specific behavior
- Use Gunner's coaching tips as the "bad cop" — "The AI flagged this, let's work on it" takes pressure off you
- Celebrate improvement publicly (leaderboard, group chat shoutouts)
- Keep coaching sessions to 15-20 min max — focused beats long

## Account Management

- Team members log in via Google auth at **getgunner.ai**
- You'll need to create/invite accounts for each team member
- Calls pull in automatically from GHL — no setup needed on their end
