# Wholesale Real Estate — Context

## What NAH Does
New Again Houses buys distressed properties below market value and assigns the contracts to cash buyers (investors). No renovation, no holding — pure deal flow.

## The Pipeline
```
Lead Gen → Initial Contact → Appointment → Offer → Contract → Assign to Buyer → Close
```

## Key Roles
- **Lead Generator (LG):** Cold calls/texts potential sellers. Gets the first conversation.
- **Lead Manager (LM):** Nurtures interested leads, sets appointments, handles follow-up.
- **Acquisition Manager (AM):** Runs the appointment, makes the offer, gets the contract signed.
- **Dispo Manager:** Takes the signed contract, finds a cash buyer, closes the deal.

## Key Metrics
- **Leads:** New contacts entered
- **Apts:** Appointments set
- **Offers:** Offers made
- **Contracts:** Signed purchase agreements
- **Closed:** Deals that funded

## Lead Sources
- BatchDialer (cold call lists)
- BatchLeads (cold SMS lists)
- PPL platforms (Leadzolo, PropertyLeads, MotivatedSellers) — pay per lead
- GHL (manages all inbound/outbound once in pipeline)

## Markets
- Nashville, TN (primary)
- Memphis, TN (secondary)

## What "Motivated Seller" Means
A property owner who needs to sell quickly — often due to foreclosure, divorce, inheritance, job loss, or property condition. They'll accept below-market offers for speed/certainty.
