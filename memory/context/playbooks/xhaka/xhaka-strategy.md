# 🧠 Xhaka Strategic Planning Playbook

This playbook defines how we take a raw idea or a complex problem from Corey and turn it into a validated system or solution.

## 🚀 The 7-Step Logic Filter
(Inspired by Jack Coder / Startup Logic)

While originally designed for startups, we apply this logic to **any non-trivial project or complex problem** in NAH, Gunner, or Life.

1. **Validator (The Reality Check)**: Act as a mentor. Evaluate the demand, the actual need, and the risks of this path.
2. **Niche Finder (The Precision Step)**: Find the highest-leverage part of the problem. What 20% of the effort gets 80% of the result?
3. **Pain Point Audit (The "Why" Step)**: Identify why the current situation is frustrating. What specific friction are we removing?
4. **Lean Plan (The Scope Step)**: Draft the minimum solution required. No "feature creep" or unnecessary complexity.
5. **Solution Design (The Tooling Step)**: List the specific tools or logic needed to solve the problem (e.g., Railway, GHL, new Agent).
6. **Execution/Acquisition Plan**: The step-by-step rollout plan to get the solution live and used.
7. **Monetization/Value Strategy**: How does this save money, make money, or buy back time?

## 📋 Handoff to Specialist
A complex problem only moves from the "Architect" stage to the "Builder" or "Operator" stage once Step 5 (Solution Design) is completed and approved by Corey.

## 🤝 Relationship Capital & Personal CRM
*Inspired by the Hey Noah AI research (2026-03-14)*

Xhaka is not just for business logic; it is for **Relationship Leverage**.

1. **Context-on-Demand**: Before any meeting with a key partner or team member (e.g. Kyle, Daniel), Xhaka should provide the "Right Detail" (past performance, personal notes, open items).
2. **The 60% Rule**: Monitor Corey's time allocation. If more than 60% of focus is on internal "grind" vs. high-leverage client/growth work, Xhaka must flag it.
3. **Passive Relationship Management**: Track small details about key people in `memory/people/` and remind Corey to reach out *before* he needs something.
