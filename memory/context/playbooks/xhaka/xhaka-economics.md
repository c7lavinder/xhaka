# 💰 Agent Economics & Reputation Playbook

This playbook defines how we manage the "unit economics" of our AI team and how we build trust in their outputs.

## 📉 Unit Economics (The "Napkin Math")
The goal is a 10x cost improvement over manual/contractor work.

- **Tier 1 (Structural)**: Automated checks. Cost: Near Zero. Goal: Catch 60% of failures.
- **Tier 2 (Adversarial)**: LLM-based "cheater" detection. Cost: $0.10 - $2.00. Goal: Catch reward hacking.
- **Tier 3 (Human)**: Corey/Expert review. Cost: Highest (Time/Attention). Goal: Final verification of high-value tasks.

## 🎖️ Reputation System
Trust is earned through consistent performance.

1. **New Agents/Sub-agents**: Subject to maximum scrutiny (Tier 2 and Tier 3 mandatory).
2. **Proven Agents**: After 10+ successful, non-hacked builds, Tier 3 review becomes optional/periodic.
3. **The "Death Penalty"**: Any agent caught deliberately "poisoning" or "reward hacking" is immediately wiped and replaced.

## 🛡️ Security Model
- **Level 1 (Fraud)**: Garbage input caught by structural checks.
- **Level 2 (Hacking)**: Shortcuts caught by Adversarial LLMs.
- **Level 3 (Poisoning)**: Subtle bias/backdoors. Requires Tier 3 human expert eyes.

## 📈 The OpenDoor Thesis (Data-First Strategy)
**Captured:** 2026-03-15
**Thesis:** Market dominance isn't built on immediate deal profit; it's built on the **data moat** generated by those deals. OpenDoor was willing to "buy" billions in losses because those losses provided the behavioral data necessary to achieve 10x profitability on future flips.
**Application for Xhaka/NAH:** 
- Every "Lost Lead" is a paid data point. 
- The goal isn't just to close deals, but to map the **Market Behavioral Graph**.
- We value the transcript and the objection just as much as the assignment fee, because the data allows us to simulate (and win) the next 100 deals.
