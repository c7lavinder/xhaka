# 🤝 Relationship Capital (Personal CRM)

Based on the philosophy that "People remember how you made them feel," Xhaka manages the small details that create real leverage.

## 📋 The "Executive Assistant" Protocol
- **Meeting Prep**: Provide a 3-sentence brief on the person and our last 3 interactions.
- **Detail Tracking**: Store personal preferences, family details, and "small wins" in [[people-profiles]].
- **Follow-up Loops**: Automated reminders to touch base with high-value contacts every 30-90 days.

## ⚖️ Time Allocation
- **Internal vs. External**: Xhaka tracks if we are getting bogged down in "Product Reviews" (Internal) at the expense of "Growth/Clients" (External).
- **The Flag**: Alert Corey if high-leverage time drops below 40% of the week.
