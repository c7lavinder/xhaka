### 1. SEO Website Auditor
**Framework (5-priority order):**
1. Crawlability & Indexation — robots.txt, XML sitemap, site architecture
2. Technical Foundations — Core Web Vitals (LCP <2.5s, INP <200ms, CLS <0.1), HTTPS, mobile
3. On-Page Optimization — title tags (50-60 chars, keyword first), H1 structure, meta descriptions
4. Content Quality — E-E-A-T signals, depth vs competitors, keyword intent match
5. Authority & Links — backlinks, credibility signals

**Gunner Application:** When onboarding a new industry vertical, run SEO Auditor against top 3 competitor sites. Output: keyword gap report + technical issue list for Gunner's landing page for that vertical.

**Output format:** Executive Summary (top 3-5 priority issues + quick wins) → Technical Findings → On-Page Findings → Prioritized Action Plan.

### 2. Copywriter Skill
**Core Principles:**
- **Clarity over cleverness** — Every sentence has one job. Remove words that don't add meaning.
- **Benefits over features** — Don't say "AI call grading." Say "Know exactly why your rep lost the deal."
- **Specificity over vagueness** — "Cut deal review time from 2 hours to 10 minutes" not "save time"
- **Customer language** — Use words your buyers use (from GHL call transcripts, Gunner feedback)
- **One idea per section** — Build a logical flow; don't cram features

**Proven Headline Formulas for Gunner:**
- `{Achieve outcome} without {pain point}` → "Close more deals without sitting through hours of call recordings"
- `Never {bad event} again` → "Never lose a deal the same way twice"
- `{Feature} for {audience}` → "AI call coaching for wholesale real estate acquisition teams"
- `Stop {pain}. Start {pleasure}.` → "Stop guessing. Start coaching with data."
- `Turn {input} into {outcome}` → "Turn your worst calls into your team's best lessons"

**CTA Rules:**
- Communicate what they GET, not what they DO
- ✅ "Get My Coaching Report" > ❌ "Sign Up"
- ✅ "See My Team's Scores" > ❌ "Submit"

**Page Structure:** Above the fold (headline + subhead + CTA) → Social proof → Problem section ("You know the feeling...") → Solution/Benefits (3-5 max) → How it Works (3-4 steps) → Second CTA

3. **Automated Ad Variation**: Generates multiple headlines and CTA versions for split testing.
4. **UI Design Reviewer**: Analyzes page structure for conversion optimization.

## 📈 Strategic Workflows
- **Phased Content Generation**:
    - Phase 1: Scripting (Hooks, Triggers, Story Structure).
    - Phase 2: High-Volume Production (Clips, Ads).
    - Phase 3: Payout/Conversion (Linking content to verified brand budget/demand).
- **Collect-then-Distribute**: Build a 60-day library of content before going live to ensure zero-gap posting.
