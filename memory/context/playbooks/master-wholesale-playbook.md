## PART 1: GENERATING LEADS (Courtney Scott)

### The Funnel
```
Meta Ad (FB/IG) → Landing Page (qualifying Qs) → CRM Auto-Entry → Auto Follow-Up (SMS+Email) → Human/AI Call → Appointment → Close
```

### Key Principles:
- **Meta > Google PPC** — "94% of people skip PPC. Meta lets us engage sellers where they actually spend time."
- **Conversion rate > traffic** — $10 CPC at 1% = $1,000/lead. At 10% = $100/lead. Fix the landing page FIRST.
- **The average person sees 40,000 ads/day** — creativity is non-negotiable. Stand out or die.
- **Full-funnel management** — ads + landing page + email + retargeting + CRM. Not just "running ads."
- **Speed to lead** — Auto-SMS fires within seconds. First human call within 5 minutes.

### Metrics to Track:
| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Cost per lead (Meta) | $5-50 |
| Cost per qualified lead | $20-100 |
| Cost per contract | $500-3,000 |
| ROAS | 3-10x+ |
| Landing page conversion | 10%+ |

### For Teams Without Ad Budget:
Use Tyson's cold calling approach (see Part 2) as the free alternative.

## PART 2: MAKING FIRST CONTACT (Tyson Smith)

### The Cold Call Script (2 sentences)
**Line 1:** "Hey, is this the owner of [ADDRESS]?"
**Line 2:** "Are you interested in selling that property?"

That's it. This script has generated millions in wholesale deals.

### Why It Works:
- Address-first throws them off → creates curiosity
- Gets to the point → more calls = more deals
- You're an INTERVIEWER, not a salesperson
- > "99% of cold calling is just finding people interested in a cash offer."

### The 3 Fundamentals (Know Before Calling):
1. **Who you are** → "Me and my partner buy properties for cash in the area"
2. **What you want** → "Looking to buy houses"
3. **Why** → "We rent them out or fix and flip"

### Common Objections:
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "How'd you get my number?" | "My partner compiled a list of areas we want to buy in" |
| "What's your offer?" | **Car Analogy:** "Have you ever test driven a car before buying? Same thing — I need to see the property first. When's good?" |
| "I don't know if I want to sell" | "Before I called, were you even considering it?" If no → move on. If yes → keep going. |

### Volume Mindset:
> "Every cold call is spinning a roulette board. Most say no. But there are slivers that say $10K, $50K, $100K. If you could spin for free all day, you'd never leave."

- **Minimum:** 1,000+ dials/day
- **8 hours/day** on the phone
- **2.5 months** average before first deal
- **Fight fire with WATER** — calm beats aggressive every time

## PART 3: QUALIFYING & FOLLOW-UP (Chandler Saine)

### The Lead Manager System
The single most impactful hire in wholesaling. Before the AM ever touches a lead:

**What the LM does:**
- Calls ALL new leads (outbound) or follows up on inbound
- Qualifies: motivated? timeline? equity? condition?
- Passes ONLY hot leads to AM
- Manages CRM — no lead falls through

**What the LM needs:**
1. A sales script
2. A process to follow  
3. A dialed-in CRM

> "Three times in 30 days, we hired a lead manager on the FIRST call. They literally 2x'd their business."

### Follow-Up Cadence:
| Lead Temp | Frequency | Who |
|-----------|-----------|-----|
| Hot (motivated + timeline) | Every 2-3 days | AM |
| Warm (interested, exploring) | Weekly | LM |
| Cold (not now, maybe later) | Bi-weekly to monthly | LM |
| Dead (firm no) | Quarterly drip | Automated |

### KPIs to Track:
**Marketing:** Cost per lead, per qualified lead, per contract, per closed deal, ROI by channel
**Sales:** Speed to lead, calls/day, conversations, appointments, offers, contracts, follow-up rate
**Financial:** Gross revenue, marketing spend, net profit, profit per deal, profit margin

> "Your first $100K month is a systems problem, not a marketing problem. Most businesses have enough leads. They just waste them."

### The Daily Optimizer (Time Audit):
1. List every task that takes your attention
2. Grade 0-3 (0 = no revenue, 3 = most revenue)
3. 0s and 1s → delegate/automate. 2s and 3s → do MORE of these.

> "4% of activities produce 64% of your outcomes."

## PART 4: CLOSING THE DEAL (Todd Toback + Steve Trang)

### Todd's 10-Step Close:

| Step | What | Key |
|------|------|-----|
| 1 | **The Pour** | Build rapport intentionally |
| 2 | **Set Agenda & Ask Permission** | Tell them how the call will go. Ask permission to ask questions. |
| 3-4 | **Qualify** | (1) Do they have a PROBLEM? (2) Will they trade equity to solve it? |
| 5 | **Review Commitments** | "What happens next? Yes or no? When do we talk?" |
| 6-8 | **Handle Objections** | Use 5 communication techniques (see below) |
| 9 | **The Offer** | LAST — after commitments + objections handled |
| 10 | **The Boomerang** | "Are you sure? Is this a good fit?" → they re-affirm emotion → prevents cancellations |

### Todd's 5 Communication Techniques:
1. **Cushioning** — "If it's not a fit, that's okay"
2. **Reversing** — Answer questions with questions
3. **Truth Telling** — Be blunt. "You'd net more listing with an agent. Maybe that's better for you." → They reveal real motivation.
4. **Stealth Mismatch** — Give the opposite of what they expect → they come back toward you
5. **Getting Commitments** — Define next step in painstaking detail

### Steve's Personality-Adaptive Selling (PMAS™):
Detect personality type in first 2-3 minutes. Adapt:

| Type | How to Sell Them |
|------|-----------------|
| **Analytical** | Facts, comps, numbers, proof |
| **Expressive** | Empathy, stories, emotional connection |
| **Driver** | Get to the point, be direct, don't waste time |
| **Amiable** | Reassure, take time, no pressure |

> "How can they object to what they've told you they wanted to buy?"

### Steve's Key Techniques:
- **Assign a cost to inaction:** "That property is costing you $X/month in taxes, maintenance, stress. Over 12 months that's $Y you're losing."
- **Pull back when they say yes:** "I'm not sure we're the best fit yet. Let me check a few things."
- **Use their own words:** "You said earlier the reason was X. And you just told me Y. So which is it?"
- **Don't sell — make THEM sell you:** > "We don't sell. We get people to sell themselves."

### Jerry Norton's 7 Seller Personas (Use 3+ Per Call):

| Persona | When to Use | Key Phrase |
|---------|------------|-----------|
| 🔬 **Analyst** | Data-driven seller | "Based on the last 3 comps..." |
| 💚 **Affirmer** | Emotional/distressed | "That must be really hard." |
| ⚡ **Challenger** | Unrealistic expectations | "Help me understand how that works." |
| 😰 **Worrier** | Indecisive/procrastinator | "What happens if this doesn't sell before foreclosure?" |
| 🎓 **Expert** | Confused about options | "If I were in your shoes, here's what I'd do..." |
| 😄 **Comedian** | Tense/guarded moments | Humor to humanize yourself |
| 🤷 **Idiot** | Seller gives high number | "Help me understand. Maybe I'm missing something." |

**Grading rule:** Perfect calls use 3+ personas. Flag calls stuck in one mode.

### The 5 Main Objections + Responses:

**1. "I want more money"**
→ Go back to pain: "What were you hoping to get?"
→ Reframe: "If speed and certainty matter more than top dollar..."
→ Todd: Double your target assignment fee. "The size of your deal is what you expect."

**2. "I need more time" (THE KILLER)**
→ Timeline Compression: "How long has this been going on? What's your drop-dead date?"
→ Get specific: "Would 30 days be fair?"
→ Condition for pushback: "What if they say no? How do we handle that?"
→ Future pace: "If this is handled, are we ready Monday?"

**3. "I want to shop around / get more offers"**
→ "That's smart. What are you looking for that other offers aren't providing?"
→ Find the gap (speed, certainty, flexibility)

**4. "I don't know your company"**
→ Reverse: "Why is that important to you?"
→ Match their concern with relevant proof

**5. "I need to think about it"**
→ "What specifically do you need to think about?"
→ Identify the REAL objection hiding behind it

### After Signing — The Friction Principle:
Don't celebrate. Slow it down.
> "The worst thing is 'Let's do it!' Because later he thinks 'I could have got more.' Then his sister calls."

## PART 5: SCALING THE MACHINE (Chandler Saine)

### The Hiring Journey:

| Revenue | Team Size | Key Hire | What Changes |
|---------|-----------|----------|-------------|
| $0-First Deal | 1 | — | You do everything |
| $15K/mo | 1-2 | **Lead Manager** | Follow-up actually happens |
| $30K/mo | 2-4 | TC/Admin/EA | Admin off your plate |
| $50K/mo | 3-6 | **Acquisition Manager** ← MOST IMPORTANT | "They IMMEDIATELY blow up to $100K" |
| $100K/mo+ | 6-10+ | 2nd AM, Dispo, Marketing | Stabilize + grow |
| $300K/mo | ~28 | Full departments | You're CEO only |

### Chandler's $300K/mo Team:
- Marketing: Director + 2 Data Mgrs + Copywriter + 15 Cold Callers (managed service)
- Lead Mgmt: Director + Assistant + 7 LMs
- Acquisitions: Working Manager + 4 AMs
- Dispo: Working Manager + Rep + Assistant
- Transactions: TC + Assistant
- Ops: IT + Bookkeeper + 2 EAs + Fractional CFO + Coach

### Profit First (Don't Skip This):
| Bucket | % of Revenue |
|--------|-------------|
| Profit | 10-15% (taken FIRST) |
| Owner's Pay | 30-40% |
| Tax | 15-20% |
| Operating Expenses | 30-40% |

> "Most wholesalers do $50K months but keep $5K. That's not a business — that's a job with worse hours."

### Red Flags:
- Marketing spend > 40% of revenue
- 2+ channels without per-channel ROI tracking
- Hiring ahead of revenue
- Revenue growing but profit flat

### The ONE Channel Rule (Todd + Chandler + Courtney agree):
> "I see this over and over — wholesalers throwing money at multiple channels with zero focus, zero measurement, zero consistency." — Todd
> "The biggest mistake is spending on 10 marketing sources." — Chandler
> "Take one channel and squeeze it dry." — Todd's RAM principle

Pick ONE. Master it. Scale it. THEN add another.

## PART 6: THE GUNNER AI APPLICATION

### How Each Coach Maps to Gunner Features:

| Gunner Feature | Primary Coach | What It Does |
|---------------|--------------|-------------|
| **Call Grading Rubric** | Todd + Steve + Jerry Norton | Grade on rapport, pain probing, persona switching, objection handling, commitment checks |
| **KPI Dashboard** | Chandler + Courtney | Marketing ROI, sales metrics, financial health, per-channel tracking |
| **Training Module** | All 5 | Tyson=Cold Call 101, Todd=Closing Mastery, Chandler=Scaling Systems, Steve=Sales Mastery, Courtney=Lead Gen |
| **Onboarding Wizard** | Chandler + Tyson | Scripts, processes, CRM setup, first-milestone tracking |
| **Lead Scoring** | Chandler + Steve | Qualification criteria, personality detection, urgency signals |
| **Follow-Up Automation** | Chandler + Courtney | Cadence by lead temp, auto-sequences, orphan scanning |
| **Team Performance** | Chandler | Per-rep metrics, role-based views, accountability chart |

### Bland.ai Voice Agent Configuration:

**Default mode:** Affirmer + Expert blend (warm, helpful, advisory)

**Personality detection (Steve's PMAS):**
- Analytical seller → switch to facts/comps
- Emotional seller → switch to empathy/validation
- Direct seller → get to the point
- Cautious seller → reassure, slow down

**Cold call opener (Tyson):**
"Hey, is this the owner of [ADDRESS]? Are you interested in selling that property?"

**Warm/inbound opener (Courtney's leads):**
"Hi [name], I see you reached out about your property at [address]. Tell me a little about what's going on."

**Qualification questions (Todd + Chandler):**
1. "What's got you thinking about selling?"
2. "What happens if you don't sell?"
3. "What's your ideal timeline?"
4. "Have you spoken with anyone else?"
5. "If we could work something out that's fair, would you be open to meeting?"

**Objection responses:** Pre-loaded with Todd's 5 techniques + Tyson's car analogy + Steve's cost-of-inaction

### Gunner Call Grading Rubric:

| Category | Weight | What to Grade |
|----------|--------|--------------|
| **Rapport Building** | 20% | Did they build trust? Listen? (Todd) |
| **Pain Probing** | 20% | Did they uncover real motivation? (Todd) |
| **Personality Adaptation** | 15% | Did they adjust to seller's type? (Steve/Jerry) |
| **Objection Handling** | 15% | Addressed or folded? Prevented or reactive? (Todd/Steve) |
| **Commitment Setting** | 15% | Clear next step defined? (Todd) |
| **Persona Switching** | 10% | Used 3+ personas? (Jerry Norton) |
| **Energy/Confidence** | 5% | Maintained posture throughout? (Tyson/Todd) |

**Auto-flags:**
- Call under 2 minutes → skip grading
- No pain probing questions asked → flag for coaching
- Lead dispositioned "not motivated" without sending offer → flag (Todd: "80% of 'unmotivated' sellers ARE motivated")
- No follow-up scheduled → flag
- Script-reading detected → flag for frameworks training (Steve)
