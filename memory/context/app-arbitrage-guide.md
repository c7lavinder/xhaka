# Research: B2C App Arbitrage (800k/yr Guide)

- **Source:** "I built 10 apps in 10 months and make $800,000/yr"
- **Key Philosophy:** Apps as "Digital Real Estate." Build once, sell forever. Focus on B2C subscriptions with hard, unskippable paywalls.

## The "Speed of Sound" Stack (2026)
- **Coding:** Cursor + Rork (AI-driven development)
- **Prompts:** ChatGPT
- **Paywalls:** Superwall (Primary) or RevenueCat
- **Database:** Firebase (if needed)
- **Design:** Pinterest/Dribbble for reference-based UI generation
- **Deployment:** Xcode (Apple Developer license required)

## The 8-Step Formula
1. **Validation:** Find a painful problem. Check the App Store. If competitors make >$10k/mo, it's validated.
2. **Onboarding is 70% of the App:** Rip the onboarding *structure* (not literal content) from top competitors. It reminds users why they downloaded and highlights the problem.
3. **Hard Paywall:** Force users to pay upfront or start a trial at the end of onboarding. "Don't be scared to ask for money."
4. **App Store Optimization (ASO):** 
   - Name must be clear. 
   - Subtitle + 1st Screenshot do all the work. 
   - Ugly/Clear > Pretty/Confusing.
5. **Marketing Channels:**
   - **UGC (User Generated Content):** Pay creators ~$15/video + massive viral bonuses (e.g., $800 for 1M views).
   - **Influencers:** Target $1 CPM deals ($1 per 1,000 views).
   - **Faceless Content:** Slideshows, screen recordings, ChatGPT-generated characters.
   - **Founder-Led:** Viral formats where the founder talks/demos.
   - **Paid Ads:** Scale winning creatives via TikTok/Meta Spark ads.

## Strategic Takeaway for Gunner
- **Onboarding:** Gunner's onboarding should be as aggressive as a B2C app—immediately showing the user their "problem" (bad sales calls) and the "solution" (AI grading).
- **Paywall Strategy:** Even for B2B, the "paywall at the end of onboarding" ensures high intent.
- **Marketing:** The "Founder-Led" and "UGC" strategies for acquisition are highly applicable to your brand.
