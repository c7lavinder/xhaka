# NAH Wholesale Operations — Full Value Chain

## The Pipeline
```
Lead Gen (Courtney/Meta) → First Contact (Tyson/Cold Call) → LM Qualifies (Chandler) → AM Closes (Todd+Steve) → Dispo (Esteban) → Close
```

## Stage 1: Lead Gen
- Meta > Google PPC
- Speed to lead: SMS within seconds, call within 5 min
- Target: $5-50 CPL, 10%+ landing page conversion

## Stage 2: First Contact
- Cold call opener: "Hey, is this the owner of [ADDRESS]? Are you interested in selling?"
- You're an INTERVIEWER not a salesperson
- Volume mindset: 1,000+ dials/day, 2.5 months avg to first deal

## Stage 3: LM Qualification
- LM calls ALL leads first, passes only hot to AM
- Hot = motivated + timeline + equity
- Follow-up cadence: Hot=every 2-3 days, Warm=weekly, Cold=bi-weekly

## Stage 4: Closing (Todd's 10 Steps)
1. Build rapport (The Pour)
2. Set agenda + ask permission
3-4. Qualify: problem? Will they trade equity?
5. Review commitments
6-8. Handle objections (cushion, reverse, truth-tell, stealth mismatch, get commitments)
9. Make the offer (LAST — after everything else)
10. The Boomerang — make them re-affirm to prevent cancellations

## Stage 5: Objection Handling
- "I want more money" → back to pain, reframe speed/certainty
- "I need more time" → compress timeline, get specific date
- "I need to think about it" → "What specifically?"
- "I want more offers" → "What are you looking for that others aren't providing?"

## Stage 6: Scaling Milestones
- $15K/mo → hire Lead Manager
- $50K/mo → hire Acquisition Manager (biggest lever)
- $100K/mo → second AM + Dispo
- $300K/mo → full departments (~28 people)

## Key Metrics
- Speed to lead, calls/day, conversations, appointments, offers, contracts
- Cost per lead, per qualified lead, per contract, per closed deal
- Profit First: 10-15% profit taken first, 30-40% owner pay, 15-20% tax, 30-40% opex

## Gunner Call Grading Weights
- Rapport: 20%
- Pain probing: 20%
- Personality adaptation: 15%
- Objection handling: 15%
- Commitment setting: 15%
- Persona switching (3+ personas): 10%
- Energy/confidence: 5%

## Auto-Flag Triggers
- Call < 2 min → skip grading
- No pain probing → flag for coaching
- No follow-up scheduled → flag
- "Not motivated" without offer sent → flag (80% of "unmotivated" ARE motivated — Todd)
