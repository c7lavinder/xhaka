# AI Value Chain — Infrastructure Gravity

## The Five Layers
1. **Energy** — power plants, grid, cooling. Low risk, steady returns. Tutorial level.
2. **Chips** — NVIDIA (92% GPU market), TSMC (70% foundry), ASML (sole EUV supplier). Boss fight. Highest margins.
3. **Cloud** — AWS/Azure/GCP. $650B capex in 2026. Everyone pays rent here.
4. **Models** — OpenAI, Anthropic, Google. Fastest revenue growth ever. Also burning fastest. Structurally unprofitable until 2029+.
5. **Applications** — ChatGPT, Copilot, your app. Most visible. Most crowded. Thinnest margins now.

## The Core Thesis
**Revenue flows up. Capital flows down.**
OpenAI earns $20B → pays 20% to Microsoft → Microsoft buys Nvidia chips → Nvidia prints 75% gross margin.
The users are at the top. The profit is at the bottom.

## Infrastructure Gravity (Historical Pattern)
Every computing platform: value concentrates in infrastructure first, applications later.
- Internet era: Cisco/Corning/fiber won first. Google/Amazon/Facebook won later.
- Electricity era: GE/Westinghouse/utilities won first. Everything else followed.
- AI era: Nvidia/TSMC/hyperscalers winning NOW. Application layer consolidates later.

## What This Means for Gunner
Gunner is Layer 5 (Applications). Correct positioning — but:
- Data moat is the only durable Layer 5 advantage
- "Companies sitting on proprietary data can fine-tune models in ways generic chatbots cannot"
- Gunner's moat = NAH call recordings + coaching outcomes + conversion data
- Every call processed = data equity, not just coaching value

## The Risks Worth Tracking
- **DeepSeek effect**: efficient models closing gap = infrastructure spend thesis weakens
- **Concentration risk**: TSMC earthquake = years of AI setback
- **Capital misallocation**: $650B capex needs revenue to follow

## Relevant Numbers (2026)
- NVIDIA revenue: $215.9B FY2026, 65% YoY growth, 75% gross margin
- TSMC: $122.5B revenue, 70% global foundry share
- OpenAI: $20B ARR, burning $17B/yr, profitable 2029 at earliest
- Hyperscaler capex: $650-700B in 2026 (up from ~$200B in 2022)
- AI data centre power: ~90 TWh/yr by 2026 (10x from 2022)
