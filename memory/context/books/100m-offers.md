# $100M Offers — Alex Hormozi

## Core Thesis
Make an offer so good people feel stupid saying no. Price is only an objection when value hasn't been communicated. The goal isn't to be cheaper — it's to make the value so obvious that price becomes irrelevant.

## Key Mental Models

### The Value Equation
Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) / (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)
- Increase: Dream Outcome, Perceived Likelihood
- Decrease: Time Delay, Effort required
- Price can go up as value equation improves

### The Grand Slam Offer
Bundle together: core offer + bonuses + guarantees + scarcity/urgency in a way that makes comparison impossible. You're not selling a commodity — you're selling a unique solution.

### Niche Down
The riches are in the niches. A generic offer competes on price. A niche offer competes on fit. "We help residential contractors write professional estimates in 10 minutes" beats "we help businesses save time."

### Bonuses > Discounts
Adding bonuses increases perceived value without cutting price. Discounting trains buyers to wait for deals and signals low confidence.

### Guarantees Reverse Risk
The seller normally holds all the risk. Guarantees transfer risk back to the seller and remove the buyer's objection. "If you don't close a deal using our coaching within 90 days, we refund everything."

## Application to NAH
- Seller offer: not "we buy houses" but "we close in 7 days, no repairs, no agents, no fees — you pick the date"
- The value equation for motivated sellers: Dream Outcome (fast cash, done) × Likelihood (we've closed 100+) / Time (7 days) × Effort (one call)
- Objection "your price is too low" → the answer is adding value (speed, certainty, convenience) not raising price

## Application to Gunner
- Gunner's offer should be framed through the value equation: Dream Outcome (team closes more deals) × Likelihood (AI-graded calls, specific feedback) / Time (see results in 30 days) × Effort (plug in GHL, done)
- The current offer is too vague — "AI call coaching" doesn't trigger the value equation
- Add a guarantee: if your team's average score doesn't improve 10 points in 60 days, refund
- Niche the positioning: "For wholesale real estate teams" not "for sales teams"

## Behavioral Rules for Xhaka
- When Corey is discussing pricing for NAH deals or Gunner, run the value equation: are we maximizing Dream Outcome and Perceived Likelihood while minimizing Time and Effort?
- When a Gunner prospect objects on price, ask: what's the offer missing? (Not: should we lower the price?)
- When evaluating Gunner's positioning, ask: is this niche enough that a residential contractor instantly recognizes themselves?
- Periodically surface: what guarantee could Gunner offer that would make saying no feel stupid?
