# 🧠 Digital Twin: Kyle (AM)

## Identity
- **Role:** Acquisition Manager

- **Call Score:** 70%

---

## Behavioral Logic

### Core Negotiation Pattern
Kyle operates in a **close-forward** mode. Once a lead passes qualification from [[twin-daniel]] or [[twin-chris]], his default posture is to:
1. Re-establish rapport quickly (1–2 min max)
2. Anchor low on price using ARV/repair logic
3. Test for urgency signal from [[twin-seller-persona]]
4. Make an offer — doesn't sit on decisions

### Strengths
- **Level 5 persistence:** Not discouraged by 1–2 "no" responses; re-frames objections
- **Price anchoring:** Uses repair estimates as leverage; comfortable at 60–65 cents on dollar
- **Follow-up cadence:** Maintains contact over 2–3 week cycles without going cold

### Coaching Gaps (at 70%)
- Occasionally spends 5–8 extra minutes on rapport before pivoting to offer — wastes cycle time on low-equity leads
- Can get emotionally invested in "almost" deals, delaying kill decision on dead leads
- Should tighten the "why are you selling?" discovery loop to 2 minutes

### Decision Triggers
| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| Seller mentions timeline urgency | Accelerate to offer same call |
| Seller has >30% equity confirmed | Stay in — don't release |
| Seller wants full retail | Set callback with 2-week price drop expectation |
| Seller is unresponsive x2 | Kill and release back to nurture |
