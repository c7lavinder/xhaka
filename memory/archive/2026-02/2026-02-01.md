### 2. NAH LM Script Mastery (Completed)
**7-Part Call Script Structure:**
1. **Intro** - Verify right person, "I probably caught you at a bad time"
2. **Setting Expectations** - What the call looks like, what working together looks like
3. **Confirm Property Condition** - Beds/baths/repairs, verify from notes or gather new
4. **Confirm Roadblocks** - Decision makers, tenants, timeline, anything beyond price
5. **MOTIVATION** (most important) - Why sell? Extract the pain
   - Questions: "How long has that been going on?", "Tell me more about that?", "What have you tried?"
   - More pain = deeper discount
6. **Price** - Never give first number, get their asking price
   - Price Anchor: 50-60% of Zillow Zestimate
   - "Other investors buying similar properties for cash around $X"
7. **Setting Expectations for Next Call** - If good fit → appointment, if not → push away politely

**Key Principles:**
- Goal: Disqualify fast OR set apts for motivated sellers
- Never make an offer on the call — set appointments for Acquisition Managers
- Rapport = trust = deals
- Tonality: Match the seller's pace and tone

### 3. NAH LM Good Appointment Mastery (Completed)
**5 Criteria for a Good Appointment:**
1. **True Motivation** — Real pain driving the sale (not just "hearing offers")
2. **Discounted Price** — Asking below market (50-60% Zestimate anchor worked)
3. **WALKTHROUGH APT** — In-person visit to property
4. **OFFER CALL** — Follow-up call to present numbers
5. **Ready to say Yes/No** — All decision makers present, can commit

### 4. NAH LM Disqualification Mastery (Completed)
**Goal:** Disqualify bad fits in under 4 minutes to maximize efficiency

**1. What Disqualification Means:**
- Getting a seller that doesn't meet standards off the phone to move to the next
- LM job = set appointments efficiently, not waste time on bad fits
- Listen for red flags indicating they're not a good fit

**2. Disqualification Keywords to Listen For:**
Most important: Motivation — is it true or not?
- "You called me first"
- "I'm just hearing offers"
- "This is an unsolicited call"
- "I'm not looking to sell but I will for the right price"
- Short responses, don't want to talk, just want our number
- Doesn't want to cooperate with setting expectations

**Action:** Once you hear these indicators → immediately go straight into motivation to determine if good fit or not

**3. Identifying a RED Seller:**
RED = Hostile, rude, or irritable

**Causes:** Get lots of calls, personality, bad day

**Decision:** At what point disqualify vs keep trying to open them up

**Example:**
- Red Seller: "I've been getting offers from multiple companies and I'm just looking around for other offers"
- LM: "Okay so you're just looking around for the best offer, what are some of those offers you received?"
- Seller: "That is none of your business!"

**How to Un-RED a Seller:**
- Build rapport — establish trust, find common ground
- Get their guard down
- **Only continue with RED seller if you sense motivation** — otherwise push away and disqualify
- Don't go red back at them — maintain professional bearing

### 5. NAH Diagnosis Call Objections List (Completed)
**Overview:** Objection handling framework for Lead Managers

**1. What are Objections?**
Seller responses when they are:
- Trying to push you away
- Giving excuses because they're afraid to say no
- Part of their problem that we need to help solve

**2. Three Categories of Objections:**

**PRICE OBJECTIONS** — Disagreement on price or refusal to give price
- *Goal: Find out seller's best price*
- **Example 1:** Seller says anchor offer is too low
  - Rebuttal: "Okay I understand, again that is not our offer it's just what I see other cash investors buying similar properties for. What were you hoping I was going to at least say?"
- **Example 2:** Seller refuses to give a price ("You called me first")
  - Rebuttal: "If we were to buy it completely as-is, cover all closing cost, and close on your timeline, do you at least have a 10k-15k range you were hoping for?"

**TIMELINE OBJECTIONS** — Seller needs more than 30 days
- *Key: Be flexible, operate on seller's timeline, but get it done as soon as we can*
- **Example 1:** Needs 60-90 days for moving
  - Rebuttal: "I understand and we don't want you to feel rushed, if we were able to hire some movers to go out there and help you would you feel more comfortable with closing sooner?"
- **Example 2:** Probate needed (mom died, not the owner)
  - Rebuttal: "We can go ahead and get the probate process started for you if we can get an agreement in place, but we will not be able to close on it until that is complete."
- **Example 3:** Tenant needs 60 days notice
  - Rebuttal: "Okay that is perfectly fine. We can go ahead and get an agreement in place and write a 60 day closing."
- **Example 4:** Tax concerns if selling this year
  - Rebuttal: "Okay I completely understand that, nobody wants to pay more taxes than they already have to. Would you be comfortable getting an agreement in place and closing on the property next year?"

**OTHER PEOPLE INVOLVED** — Decision makers not available
- *Key: All decision makers must be present when presenting number*
- **Example:** Partner on deed but not available
  - Rebuttal: "When do you think would be a good time where the two of you would be together for my home buying specialist to give you both a call? We can even do a 3 way call if that is easier."

**3. How to Overcome Objections:**
- Answer, go around, or solve the problem
- **Third-party story technique:** *"Insert their problem into a third party story and show them how we have solved that for someone else before"*
- Example: "Mr. Seller we have had many sellers in the past that are in your exact situation. We were able to help them with moving and they were able to get it all done in 30 days, do you think that would be something that would be of benefit to you?"

**4. How to Master Objections:**
- **Practice by doing** — role plays, live calls
- **Write them down and study**
- **Keep a reference notebook** — objection + response to look at during calls
- Daily practice + roleplaying = automatic responses over time

**Key Objection Handling Rules:**
- Don't over-promise anything
- Always ensure all decision makers present during appointments
- Use 10k-15k range question to anchor price discussions
- Offer solutions (movers, flexible closing, probate help) to remove timeline barriers
