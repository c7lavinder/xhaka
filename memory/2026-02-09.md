## Agent #17 Redefined — Callback Capture Bot (8:04 PM)

**Replaces:** Voicemail Bot

**Why:** Team manually listens to hundreds of callbacks per week. Massive time sink.

**Source:** CallRail — all inbound calls (answered + voicemails) from cold outreach callbacks
- People calling back BatchDialer/BatchLeads numbers
- NOT PPL or marketing inbound

**Trigger:** CallRail webhook on call completion

**Process:**
1. AI listens to recording
2. Classifies: lead or not a lead
3. If lead → extracts: name, phone, address (if given), intent
4. Creates Opportunity in GHL "Sales Process" pipeline → "New Lead" stage
5. GHL automations trigger from there (no task creation needed)

**Edge cases:**
- Caller says "I have something to sell" but no address → still creates opportunity (phone + name, address blank)
- Not a lead → **no action, completely ignored** (no logging, no review queue)

**Volume:** Hundreds per week

**Priority:** HIGH — eliminates significant manual labor
