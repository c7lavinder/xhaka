## Key Players

- **8 AI agents** built specifically for real estate investors
- **Voice agents:** Call Answer AI (24/7 inbound), Speed-To-Lead AI (instant outbound to web leads), Voice Follow AI (re-engages cold leads)
- **Non-voice agents:** Call Grader AI, Lead Score AI (150+ data points), Meet Grade AI, Conversational AI (text), KPI Insights AI
- **Pricing:** 30-day free trial, pricing not public
- **Why it matters:** Most complete AI agent suite purpose-built for RE wholesalers. Direct competitor to any Gunner expansion into the wholesaler workflow.

Source: [REsimpli blog](https://resimpli.com/blog/best-ai-agents-for-real-estate-investors/)

## Market Signal

1. **Speed-to-lead is the new bar.** In 2026, the RE businesses closing the most deals respond to every lead within 60 seconds. AI makes this possible without staffing.
2. **The stack is consolidating.** REsimpli ships 8 agents in one platform. DealMachine bundles property intel + dialer. Standalone tools lose to integrated suites.
3. **AI voice follow-up is the killer feature.** Both Ylopo (90-day auto-follow) and REsimpli (Voice Follow AI) automate the grind of persistent outreach that closes wholesale deals.
4. **Call grading is embedded, not separate.** REsimpli's Call Grader AI is a feature inside the CRM, not a standalone product. This is the direction the market is moving.

## Why This Matters

**For NAH:** These tools represent the automation layer NAH should be evaluating. REsimpli's agent suite is the closest to a turnkey AI wholesaling stack.

**For Gunner:** The RE wholesaling vertical is adopting AI calling tools fast. Gunner's call coaching angle is differentiated — none of these tools focus on making the *human caller* better. They focus on replacing the human. Gunner's bet is that coached humans outperform AI callers on high-value conversations. That bet needs validation.
