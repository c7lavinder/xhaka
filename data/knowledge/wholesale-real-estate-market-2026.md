## Market Snapshot

### Investment Activity
- CRE investment expected to increase **16% in 2026 to $562B**, nearly matching pre-pandemic (2015-2019) average
- Transaction volume in 2025 exceeded $560B, confirming recovery trend
- Office deal volume jumped **~17% YoY**, driven by flight to quality (Class A assets)

Sources: [CBRE Outlook 2026](https://www.cbre.com/insights/books/us-real-estate-market-outlook-2026), [JP Morgan](https://www.jpmorgan.com/insights/global-research/real-estate/us-housing-market-outlook)

### Sector Performance
| Sector | Signal | Trend |
|--------|--------|-------|
| **Industrial/Logistics** | Renewed demand for warehouses | Up |
| **Multifamily** | Housing shortage driving rental demand, financing available | Up |
| **Data Centers** | Deal volumes surged **37%** | Fastest-growing |
| **Specialty Property** | 14% of 2025 deal volume (senior housing, student housing, flex industrial) | Growing share since 2023 |
| **Office** | Flight to quality — Class A up, lower tiers struggling | Mixed |

Source: [Cushman & Wakefield](https://www.cushmanwakefield.com/en/united-states/insights/trends-to-watch)

### Housing Market
- Housing shortage continues — structural undersupply
- Home prices stabilizing but affordability remains challenged
- Rental demand strong across markets

Sources: [Zillow](https://www.zillow.com/home-values/102001/united-states/), [RealWealth](https://realwealth.com/learn/housing-market-predictions/)

## Technology Integration
- AI improving property valuation, market analysis, and investment strategies
- Speed-to-lead (sub-60-second response) becoming the operational bar
- AI voice agents handling initial lead qualification at scale

Source: [PwC Emerging Trends](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/industries/financial-services/asset-wealth-management/real-estate/emerging-trends-in-real-estate-pwc-uli.html)

## Why This Matters

**For NAH:**
1. **Market is recovering.** Investment volumes approaching pre-pandemic levels means more deal flow, more motivated sellers, and more competition for deals. Speed and consistency matter more than ever.
2. **Housing shortage = wholesale opportunity.** Persistent undersupply means distressed/off-market properties remain valuable. The wholesale arbitrage is alive.
3. **Industrial/logistics demand** creates opportunities for investors who can source warehouse and flex-industrial properties.

**For Gunner:**
1. **More activity = more calls.** Rising transaction volumes mean wholesalers are making more calls, creating more demand for call coaching tools.
2. **Technology adoption is accelerating** in RE. The industry is moving past "AI skepticism" into active adoption. Gunner's market timing is favorable.
