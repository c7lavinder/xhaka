## Market Context

- Global SaaS market: **$375.57B in 2026**, projected $1,482B by 2034 (18.7% CAGR)
- Driven by: AI-enabled SaaS adoption, vertical SaaS growth, enterprise cloud migration, rising M&A consolidation
- The era of **growth-at-all-costs is over.** 2026 is the era of **Efficient Growth** — balancing growth with profitability.

## Three Patterns That Matter for Gunner

### 1. Product-Led Growth → Product-Led Sales (Hybrid)

Pure self-serve PLG struggles with complex enterprise deals. The winning 2026 model:
- **Product is the qualification tool.** Usage patterns identify high-potential users.
- **Product automatically flags users for sales.** No manual lead scoring.
- **Sales closes the enterprise deal.** The product already proved value.

**Benchmarks:** Top PLG companies have NRR >120%. Leaders: Slack 140%+, Snowflake 150%+.

**For Gunner:** If Gunner has a self-serve tier, instrument it to surface power users to sales. Don't choose between PLG and sales-led — do both.

### 2. Vertical SaaS Is Winning

Industry-specific tools are growing **2-3x faster** than general productivity tools.

**Vertical SaaS 2.0 = "compound workflows"** — integrated platforms solving multiple interconnected problems for a single industry. Example: a single platform for a craft brewery that manages sales, inventory, compliance, and distribution.

Benefits: faster onboarding, better adoption, more actionable insights, less customization needed.

**For Gunner:** Gunner IS vertical SaaS (call coaching for RE wholesalers). This is the fastest-growing SaaS category. The playbook: go deep on wholesaler workflows, not wide on generic sales coaching.

### 3. AI-Native Features Are Expected

AI is no longer a differentiator — it's table stakes. The differentiator is **domain-specific AI** that understands the vertical's language, objections, and workflows.

**For Gunner:** Generic call transcription is commodity. Wholesaler-specific call grading (understanding motivated seller signals, objection patterns, deal qualification) is the moat.

## Why This Matters

Gunner sits at the intersection of two mega-trends: vertical SaaS growth (2-3x faster) and AI-native product expectations. The strategic imperative is clear:
1. Go deep on RE wholesaler workflows (not generic sales)
2. Build product-led sales motion (free tier → usage signals → sales close)
3. Make AI features domain-specific (wholesaler objections, not generic talk ratios)
