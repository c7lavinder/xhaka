## 8.1 LM New Lead Script

### Purpose
First call to a new lead — qualify interest, set appointments.

### Structure
1. **Intro** — Verify right person, "caught you at a bad time"
2. **Set Expectations** — What the call looks like
3. **Confirm Property** — Beds/baths/condition
4. **Confirm Roadblocks** — Decision makers, tenants, timeline
5. **MOTIVATION** — Why sell? (most important)
6. **Price** — Get their number first (anchor: 50-60% Zestimate)
7. **Next Steps** — Set apt or schedule follow-up

### Key Principles
- Never make an offer on the call
- Goal: Disqualify fast OR set appointment
- More pain = deeper discount potential

📄 **Full script:** `scripts/lm-new-lead-script.md`

## 8.2 Cold Caller Script

### Purpose
Initial outreach to cold lists — generate warm leads for LM team.

### Structure
1. **Opener** — Quick intro, reason for call
2. **Interest Check** — Are they open to an offer?
3. **Basic Qualify** — Property details, timeline
4. **Handoff** — Transfer to LM or schedule callback

### Key Principles
- Volume game — make lots of calls
- Quick disqualify on hard no's
- Warm transfers > callbacks

📄 **Full script:** `scripts/lg-cold-caller-script.md`

## 8.3 AM Offer Call Script

### Purpose
Present offers and negotiate with qualified sellers.

### Structure
1. **Rapport** — Check in, recap walkthrough
2. **Present Numbers** — ARV, repairs, offer
3. **Handle Objections** — Price, timeline, terms
4. **Negotiate** — Find middle ground
5. **Close** — Get commitment or clear next step

### Key Principles
- Know your numbers cold
- Don't negotiate against yourself
- Silence is powerful after presenting offer

📄 **Full script:** `scripts/am-offer-call-script.md`

## 8.8 Offer Calculator

### Purpose
Worksheet for calculating offer prices.

### Formula
```
MAO = ARV × 0.70 - Repairs - Assignment Fee

Where:
- MAO = Maximum Allowable Offer
- ARV = After Repair Value
- 0.70 = 70% rule (adjust for market)
- Repairs = Estimated rehab cost
- Assignment Fee = Your profit ($10-20K typical)
```

### Example
```
ARV: $200,000
× 0.70 = $140,000
- Repairs: $30,000
- Assignment Fee: $15,000
= MAO: $95,000
```

📄 **Full template:** `templates/offer-calculator.md`
