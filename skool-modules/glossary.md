# NAH Glossary
**Purpose:** Terms and acronyms you'll hear daily
**Status:** Draft — Feb 3, 2026

---

## Roles & People

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **AM** | Acquisition Manager — does walkthroughs and makes offers |
| **LM** | Lead Manager — qualifies leads and sets appointments |
| **Dispo** | Disposition — selling deals to buyers |
| **Data Manager** | Handles lead cleanup and KPI tracking |
| **Cold Caller** | Makes outbound calls to generate leads |
| **TC** | Title Company — handles closing |

---

## Deal Stages

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **New Lead** | Just entered system, not yet cleaned |
| **Warm Lead** | Cleaned, some interest, < 3 qualifying factors |
| **Hot Lead** | Cleaned, strong interest, 3+ qualifying factors |
| **Apt Scheduled** | Walkthrough appointment booked |
| **Made Offer** | Offer presented to seller |
| **Under Contract (UC)** | Signed purchase agreement |
| **Purchased/Closed** | Deal completed, money changed hands |

---

## Follow-Up Buckets

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **1-Month** | Motivated, close to ready, call frequently |
| **4-Month** | Interested but needs time, call occasionally |
| **12-Month** | Not motivated now, minimal effort |
| **Ghosted** | No response after 10-14 days |
| **DNW** | Do Not Work — removed from active list |
| **DNC** | Do Not Call — requested removal |

---

## Property & Deal Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **ARV** | After Repair Value — what it's worth fixed up |
| **MAO** | Maximum Allowable Offer — highest you can pay |
| **Comps** | Comparable sales — similar properties sold recently |
| **Rehab** | Repairs/renovation needed |
| **Spread** | Difference between your buy price and sell price |
| **Assignment Fee** | Your profit when assigning contract to buyer |
| **Assignment** | Selling your contract rights to a buyer |
| **Double Close** | Two separate closings (you buy, then sell) |
| **Wholesale** | Buying and quickly reselling for profit |
| **SFR** | Single Family Residence |
| **Multi** | Multi-family property (duplex, triplex, etc.) |

---

## Buyer Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Tier A** | Priority buyer — fast, funded, proven |
| **Tier B** | Qualified buyer — verified, good response |
| **Tier C** | Unverified buyer — slow, hasn't closed |
| **Buybox** | Buyer's criteria (what they want to buy) |
| **Blast** | Mass message to buyers about a deal |
| **JV** | Joint Venture — splitting a deal with partner |
| **Flipper** | Buyer who renovates and resells |
| **Landlord** | Buyer who holds as rental |
| **Turn Key** | Move-in ready property |

---

## Sales & Calling Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Anchor** | First price mentioned (sets expectations) |
| **Zestimate** | Zillow's automated value estimate |
| **Motivation** | Why the seller wants/needs to sell |
| **Pain** | The problem driving the sale |
| **Disqualify** | Determine lead isn't a fit, move on |
| **RED Seller** | Hostile/rude seller |
| **Objection** | Pushback from seller during call |
| **Rapport** | Trust/connection with seller |
| **Decision Maker** | Person who can say yes (everyone on deed) |
| **Callback** | Scheduled follow-up call |
| **Drip Campaign** | Automated text/email sequence |

---

## Tools & Systems

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **GHL** | GoHighLevel — main CRM |
| **MasterSuite** | Property evaluation software |
| **BatchLeads** | List building and skip tracing |
| **Gunner** | AI call grading platform |
| **Pipeline** | Visual stages a deal moves through |
| **Opportunity** | A deal record in GHL |
| **Contact** | A person record in GHL |
| **Workflow** | Automated sequence in GHL |
| **Tag** | Label applied to contacts/opportunities |
| **Skip Trace** | Finding contact info from property records |

---

## Metrics & KPIs

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **KPI** | Key Performance Indicator — metrics that matter |
| **Conversion Rate** | % that move to next stage |
| **Cost Per Lead** | Marketing spend ÷ leads generated |
| **Cost Per Contract** | Total spend ÷ contracts signed |
| **ROI** | Return on Investment |
| **Show Rate** | % of scheduled apts that happen |
| **Speed to Lead** | Time from lead entry to first contact |

---

## Communication

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Google Chat** | Team messaging platform |
| **@mention** | Tagging someone in chat for attention |
| **Apt Alert** | Notification when appointment is set |
| **Internal Notification** | Alert to team (not to seller) |

---

## Process Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Walkthrough** | In-person property visit |
| **Offer Call** | Phone call to present numbers |
| **Showing** | Buyer visit to property |
| **Probate** | Legal process after owner dies |
| **Title** | Legal ownership of property |
| **Clear Title** | No liens or ownership issues |
| **Closing** | Final transaction completing the sale |
| **Earnest Money** | Deposit showing buyer commitment |
