## 4.2 Phase 5: Walkthrough

### Pre-Walkthrough Flow
```
Stage 2 Completed (LM hands off)
    ↓
[AUTOMATION] Pending Apt (Carrot/Batch)
    ↓
Perform Walkthrough
```

### Before You Go
Review in GHL:
- [ ] Property address
- [ ] Seller name and contact
- [ ] Access instructions (gate code, dogs, etc.)
- [ ] LM notes on motivation and timeline
- [ ] Any special circumstances

### At the Walkthrough

#### 1. Build Rapport (First 5 min)
```
"Hey [NAME], I'm Kyle with New Again Houses. Thanks for having me out.
Before I take a look around, tell me a little about the property 
and what's going on with your situation."
```

#### 2. Property Evaluation (10-15 min)
Walk through and assess:
- **Structure:** Foundation, roof, walls
- **Systems:** HVAC, electrical, plumbing
- **Interior:** Floors, walls, kitchen, baths
- **Exterior:** Siding, landscaping, garage
- **Red flags:** Water damage, mold, major repairs

#### 3. Take Notes/Photos ⚠️ REQUIRED
Document:
- Overall condition (scale 1-10)
- Major repairs needed
- Approximate repair costs
- Anything unusual

**Photos are mandatory** — every room, exterior, and problem areas. No photos = walkthrough didn't happen. Dispo needs these for buyer packages.

#### 4. Discuss Next Steps (Last 5 min)
```
"Thanks for showing me around. I've got what I need to put together numbers.
I'll call you [TODAY/TOMORROW] at [TIME] to discuss what we can offer.
Does that work for you?"
```

### Walkthrough Outcomes
```
Perform Walkthrough
    ↓
Show Up? (decision)
    ├── YES → Evaluate → Schedule Offer Call
    └── NO → Reschedule or mark as No Show
```

### Post-Walkthrough — What Happens
```
Walkthrough Complete
    ↓
[AUTOMATION] Post Walkthrough Autoconfirmation
    ↓
Stage 3 Walkthrough Completed ✓
    ↓
Schedule Offer Call
```

## 4.3 Property Analysis

### What You're Calculating
1. **ARV (After Repair Value)** — What's it worth fixed up?
2. **Repair Costs** — How much to get it there?
3. **MAO (Maximum Allowable Offer)** — Highest you can pay

### The Formula
```
MAO = ARV × 0.70 - Repairs - Assignment Fee
```

**Example:**
- ARV: $200,000
- Repairs: $30,000  
- Target assignment fee: $15,000
- MAO = $200,000 × 0.70 - $30,000 - $15,000 = **$95,000**

### Running Comps
1. Find 3-5 comparable sales (same area, similar size)
2. Adjust for differences (bed/bath, sqft, condition)
3. Average to get ARV
4. Document your analysis

### Repair Estimation
| Category | Low | Medium | High |
|----------|-----|--------|------|
| Cosmetic (paint, carpet) | $5-10k | $10-20k | $20-30k |
| Kitchen | $5-10k | $15-25k | $30-50k |
| Bathrooms (each) | $3-5k | $5-10k | $10-20k |
| Roof | $5-8k | $8-12k | $12-20k |
| HVAC | $3-5k | $5-8k | $8-15k |
| Foundation | $5-10k | $10-25k | $25-50k+ |

## 4.4 Phase 6: Offer Process

### Offer Call Flow
```
Stage 3 Completed
    ↓
Call Seller at scheduled call time
    ↓
Accepted? (ready to talk offer)
    ├── YES → Perform Offer Call
    └── NO → Pending Apt Communication (reschedule)
```

### Offer Call Script

#### Opening
```
"Hey [NAME], it's Kyle with New Again Houses. How are you doing today?

I had a chance to run the numbers after seeing the property, 
and I'm ready to discuss what we can offer. You got a few minutes?"
```

#### Presenting the Offer
```
"So based on the condition of the property and what similar homes are selling for, 
we can offer [$AMOUNT].

This is a cash offer. We buy it as-is — you don't have to fix anything. 
We can close in as little as [TIMEFRAME], and we cover all the closing costs.

How does that sound to you?"
```

#### The Decision Point
```
Perform Offer Call
    ↓
Outcome? (decision)
    ├── ACCEPTED → Contract Call → Send to TC ✅
    ├── NEGOTIATION → Made Offer Negotiation → Decision on Price/Terms
    ├── NEEDS TIME → Pending Apt Communication
    └── REJECTED → Follow Up (Hot/Warm/Cold)
```

### Handling Responses

#### "That's lower than I expected"
```
"I hear you — help me understand what number you were hoping for?
(Listen)
Okay, you're at $X and we're at $Y. Here's the thing — 
[explain value: speed, as-is, certainty]. 
What if we could meet at $Z? Would that work?"
```

#### "I need to think about it"
```
"Of course. What specifically do you need to think through?
(Listen)
Got it. What would help you feel confident moving forward?
Can I call you back [TOMORROW/SPECIFIC TIME]?"
```

#### "Let me talk to [spouse/family]"
```
"Absolutely. When will you be able to discuss with them?
(Listen)  
Perfect — I'll call you on [DAY]. 
Would it help if I sent over a summary of the offer in writing?"
```

#### "Yes, let's do it"
```
"Great! I'll get the contract over to you today. 
We'll need signatures from [NAMES ON TITLE].
What's the best email to send it to?
I'll walk you through it once you have it open."
```

## 4.5 Negotiation

### When to Negotiate
```
Made Offer Negotiation
    ↓
Decision on Price and terms
    ├── Agreed → Contract
    ├── Counter → Evaluate and respond
    └── Too far apart → Follow Up or Walk
```

### Negotiation Levers
| Lever | Give | Get |
|-------|------|-----|
| Price | Go higher | Faster close |
| Timeline | Extend close | Lower price |
| Earnest Money | Increase deposit | Better terms |
| Contingencies | Remove inspection | Price reduction |
| Seller Rent-back | Allow post-close stay | Motivated seller |

### When to Go Higher
- Seller has legitimate competing offer
- Property is cleaner than expected
- Fast close has real value to them
- Deal still makes sense at higher price

### When to Walk Away
- Numbers don't work even at ceiling
- Seller expectations are unrealistic
- Red flags on title/condition
- Better opportunities in pipeline

### Contract Flow
```
Decision on Price and terms → Agreed
    ↓
Contract Call ✓
    ↓
Send to TC (Title Company)
    ↓
DEAL COMPLETE ✅
```

### Pre-Contract Checklist
- [ ] Final price confirmed with seller
- [ ] All decision makers identified
- [ ] Property address and legal description verified
- [ ] Closing timeline agreed
- [ ] Special terms noted (rent-back, inclusions, etc.)

### Contract Call
```
"[NAME], I've got the contract ready. Let me walk you through the key points:

- Purchase price: $[AMOUNT]
- Closing date: [DATE]
- We buy as-is, you don't fix anything
- We pay closing costs
- [ANY SPECIAL TERMS]

I'll send it over now. You can sign electronically — 
takes about 5 minutes. Ready?"
```

### After Signature
```
Contract Signed
    ↓
├── Update GHL → "Under Contract"
├── Notify team via Google Chat
├── Send contract to Title Company
├── Begin dispo handoff
```

### Send to Title Company
What TC needs:
- Signed purchase agreement
- Seller contact info
- Property details
- Target closing date
- Any special instructions

## 4.7 Follow-Up Paths (When Not Accepted)

### From the Process Map
```
Offer Not Accepted
    ↓
├── Accept or Follow Up? → FOLLOW UP
    ↓
    Follow Up Apt Prepare
    ↓
    ├── Warm Follow Up — Still interested, needs time
    ├── Cold Follow Up — Low interest, long nurture
```

### Warm Follow Up
- **Who:** Didn't accept but didn't reject
- **Cadence:** Call in 3-7 days
- **Goal:** Address concerns, potentially re-offer

### Cold Follow Up
- **Who:** Rejected or went silent after offer
- **Cadence:** Monthly touch
- **Goal:** Stay top of mind if situation changes

### Re-Offer Situations
- Seller's competing deal fell through
- Enough time has passed
- Market conditions changed
- Seller reached back out
