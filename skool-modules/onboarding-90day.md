# 90-Day Onboarding Plan
**Purpose:** Structured ramp-up for new team members
**Status:** Draft — Feb 3, 2026

---

## How to Use This

1. **Find your role section** below
2. **Check off items** as you complete them
3. **Don't skip steps** — each builds on the last
4. **Ask questions** — better to ask than guess

## Lead Manager (LM) — 90-Day Plan

### Week 1: Foundation
**Goal:** Understand the business and tools

| Day | Focus | Tasks |
|-----|-------|-------|
| 1 | Orientation | Read Quick-Start Guide, meet team, get tool access |
| 2 | Tools | Learn GHL navigation, pipeline stages, contact records |
| 3 | Process | Read Module 3 (LM), understand lead flow |
| 4 | Scripts | Study new lead script, practice out loud |
| 5 | Shadow | Listen to experienced LM calls (minimum 10) |

**End of Week 1:** Can navigate GHL, know the script, understand the process

### Week 2: Guided Practice
**Goal:** Start making calls with support

| Day | Focus | Tasks |
|-----|-------|-------|
| 6-7 | Assisted Calls | Make calls with script visible, supervisor listening |
| 8-9 | Solo Calls | Make calls independently, debrief after |
| 10 | Review | Review your calls in Gunner, identify patterns |

**End of Week 2:** Making 15+ calls/day, set 1+ appointment

### Week 3-4: Building Volume
**Goal:** Increase call volume and consistency

| Focus | Tasks |
|-------|-------|
| Volume | Ramp to 20+ calls/day |
| Follow-ups | Start working follow-up pool |
| Objections | Study objection library, practice responses |
| Appointments | Target 2-3 appointments/week |

**End of Week 4:** Consistently hitting 20 calls/day, 2+ apts/week

### Month 2: Independence
**Goal:** Own your pipeline

| Focus | Tasks |
|-------|-------|
| Full Pipeline | Manage new leads + follow-up pool independently |
| Quality | Improve show rate (appointments that happen) |
| Efficiency | Faster disqualification of bad fits |
| KPIs | Track your own numbers, identify weak spots |

**End of Month 2:** Consistent 3+ apts/week, 70%+ show rate

### Month 3: Mastery
**Goal:** Become a top performer

| Focus | Tasks |
|-------|-------|
| Advanced Objections | Handle any pushback smoothly |
| Mentoring | Help newer team members |
| Optimization | Refine your approach based on data |
| Consistency | Hit targets every week |

**End of Month 3:** Reliable performer, minimal supervision needed

## Acquisition Manager (AM) — 90-Day Plan

### Week 1: Foundation
**Goal:** Understand deals and numbers

| Day | Focus | Tasks |
|-----|-------|-------|
| 1 | Orientation | Read Quick-Start Guide, meet team, get tool access |
| 2 | Tools | Learn GHL, MasterSuite basics |
| 3 | Process | Read Module 4 (AM), understand deal flow |
| 4 | Numbers | Study offer calculator, practice deal math |
| 5 | Shadow | Ride along on walkthrough with experienced AM |

**End of Week 1:** Understand the numbers, know what a good deal looks like

### Week 2: Guided Practice
**Goal:** Do walkthroughs with support

| Day | Focus | Tasks |
|-----|-------|-------|
| 6-7 | Assisted Walkthroughs | Go on walkthroughs with supervisor |
| 8-9 | Property Analysis | Practice running numbers on past deals |
| 10 | Scripts | Study offer call script, role play |

**End of Week 2:** Can assess a property, run numbers, know the script

### Week 3-4: First Deals
**Goal:** Complete walkthroughs and make offers

| Focus | Tasks |
|-------|-------|
| Solo Walkthroughs | Do walkthroughs independently |
| Offer Calls | Make offer calls (supervised first) |
| Negotiation | Practice handling counters |
| First Contract | Target first signed contract |

**End of Week 4:** Completed 5+ walkthroughs, made 3+ offers, 1 contract

### Month 2: Volume & Consistency
**Goal:** Maintain deal flow

| Focus | Tasks |
|-------|-------|
| Weekly Rhythm | 3-5 walkthroughs/week |
| Offer Ratio | Make offer on 50%+ of walkthroughs |
| Contract Rate | Close 25%+ of offers |
| Follow-up | Work rejected offers back through system |

**End of Month 2:** 2+ contracts/month, consistent pipeline

### Month 3: Optimization
**Goal:** Maximize conversion and profit

| Focus | Tasks |
|-------|-------|
| Better Deals | Improve deal selection (higher margin) |
| Negotiation | Close more at better prices |
| Speed | Faster from walkthrough to contract |
| Independence | Minimal oversight needed |

**End of Month 3:** Reliable 2-3 contracts/month, strong margins
