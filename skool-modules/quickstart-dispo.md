# Dispo Quick-Start Guide
**Role:** Disposition Manager
**Your Job:** Sell contracted properties to buyers
**Day 1 Read Time:** 10 minutes

---

## Your Mission
Get deals under contract → Blast to buyers → Get offers → Close with buyer → Collect assignment fee

You're the exit strategy. No buyer = no payday.

---

## The Numbers
- **24-48 hours** to blast new deal
- **5-10 buyer responses** per deal (goal)
- **70%+ close rate** on deals under contract
- **$10-20K** typical assignment fee

---

## Your Daily Flow

### Morning
1. Check Dispo Pipeline — any new deals from AM?
2. Review pending buyer responses
3. Follow up on scheduled showings

### When New Deal Comes In
1. Gather deal details from AM
2. Prepare blast materials
3. Send to buyer list
4. Track responses

### Ongoing
1. Schedule buyer showings
2. Collect and compare offers
3. Negotiate best terms
4. Coordinate with title company

### End of Day
1. Update deal statuses in GHL
2. Follow up on quiet deals
3. Prep tomorrow's showings

---

## New Deal Checklist

When a deal hits "Under Contract":

```
1. Get from AM:
   - Property address
   - Contract price
   - ARV estimate
   - Repair estimate
   - Photos (if available)
   - Access instructions
   - Contract expiration date

2. Prepare blast:
   - Property summary
   - Deal numbers
   - Asking price (contract + assignment)

3. Send to buyers:
   - Priority (Tier A) first
   - Then Qualified (Tier B)
   - JV partners if needed

4. Track responses in GHL
```

---

## Buyer Pipeline

| Stage | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| Priority | Tier A — fast, funded, proven |
| Qualified | Tier B — verified, good response |
| JV Partners | Split deals, trusted partners |
| Not Qualified | Tire-kickers, slow, unverified |

**Always blast Priority first.** They close fastest.

---

## Buyer Tiers

| Tier | Criteria |
|------|----------|
| A | Verified funding, fast response, has purchased before |
| B | Qualified, decent response, newer buyer |
| C | Unverified, slow, hasn't closed |

**Focus energy on A and B.** C buyers are backup only.

---

## The Blast

What to include:
1. **Property address** (or general area for privacy)
2. **Property type** (SFR, multi, etc.)
3. **Beds/Baths/SqFt**
4. **ARV**
5. **Repair estimate**
6. **Asking price**
7. **Photos** (if available)
8. **How to respond** (text, call, form)

**Speed wins.** First 24-48 hours get most responses.

---

## Handling Buyer Responses

```
Buyer responds
    ↓
Verify they're qualified
    ↓
Answer questions
    ↓
Schedule showing (if they want to see it)
    ↓
Collect offer
    ↓
Compare all offers
    ↓
Accept best one
    ↓
Get buyer under contract
    ↓
Coordinate closing
```

---

## Showings

### Before
1. Confirm with seller (access)
2. Confirm with buyer (time)
3. Send access instructions

### During
- Be available by phone for questions
- Buyer inspects on their own (usually)

### After
1. Follow up: "What'd you think?"
2. Push for offer if interested
3. Note feedback for future

---

## Closing the Deal

| Step | Owner |
|------|-------|
| Buyer under contract | You |
| Title company engaged | You + AM |
| Clear title issues | Title company |
| Schedule closing | Title company |
| Collect assignment fee | Title company |
| Celebrate | Everyone |

**Assignment vs Double Close:**
- **Assignment** = Simpler, faster, lower fees
- **Double Close** = When spread is large or buyer can't see your fee

## Common Issues

| Problem | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| No buyer interest | Reprice, re-blast, try JV |
| Buyer ghosts | Follow up 3x, then move on |
| Title issues | Work with title company, keep seller informed |
| Contract expiring | Extend or hustle to close |
