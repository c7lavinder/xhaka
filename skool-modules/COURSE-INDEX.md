## 📊 Process Overview

```
LEAD GENERATION (Phase 1)
    │
    ▼
NEW LEAD → Data Manager cleans → Warm/Hot column
    │
    ▼
LEAD MANAGEMENT (Phases 2-4)
    │
    ├── Research & Qualify
    ├── Initial Contact
    └── Set Appointment
    │
    ▼
🟢 APPOINTMENT SCHEDULED
    │
    ▼
ACQUISITIONS (Phases 5-7)
    │
    ├── Walkthrough
    ├── Offer Call
    └── Contract
    │
    ▼
🟢 CONTRACT SIGNED
    │
    ▼
DISPOSITION
    │
    ├── Blast to Buyers
    ├── Showings
    ├── Buyer Under Contract
    └── Close with Title Company
    │
    ▼
💰 DEAL CLOSED

[80-90% of leads → FOLLOW UP → loop back to Contact]
```
