## 5.1 Dispo Role Overview

### What Dispo Does
Disposition is the exit strategy. Once we have a property under contract, Dispo finds a buyer and closes the deal. This is where the company makes money.

### Daily Responsibilities
1. Review new deals coming from Acquisitions
2. Market properties to buyer list
3. Field buyer inquiries
4. Schedule and coordinate showings
5. Negotiate with buyers
6. Manage deals through closing
7. Update buyer database

### KPIs You're Measured On
| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Deals Marketed | Properties sent to buyers |
| Buyer Responses | Inquiries received |
| Assignments | Contracts assigned to buyers |
| Closing Speed | Days from UC to close |
| Profit | Assignment fee collected |

## 5.2 Buyer Pipeline

### Pipeline Stages
Buyers move through stages based on their quality and relationship:

| Stage | Description | Action |
|-------|-------------|--------|
| **Priority** | Top buyers — close fast, pay full ask | Send deals first |
| **Qualified** | Proven buyers — have closed before | Send all relevant deals |
| **JV Partner** | Partnership deals — we split profits | Special deal structure |
| **Not Qualified** | Unproven or problem buyers | Lower priority, verify funds |

### What Makes a Priority Buyer
- Has closed with us before
- Responds quickly
- Pays asking price (or close)
- Has verified funds ready
- Professional to work with

### Moving Buyers Between Stages
- **New → Qualified:** After first successful close
- **Qualified → Priority:** Consistent, high-value closes
- **Any → Not Qualified:** Backs out, lowballs, problems

## 5.3 Buyer Tagging SOP

### Required Tags
Every buyer contact should have:

1. **Buyer Stage Tag**
   - `Priority Buyer`
   - `Qualified Buyer`
   - `JV Partner`
   - `Not Qualified`

2. **Buy Criteria Tags**
   - `Nashville` / `Davidson County` (location)
   - `SFR` / `Multi` (property type)
   - `$0-100k` / `$100-200k` / `$200k+` (price range)
   - `Rehab` / `Turnkey` (condition)

3. **Activity Tags**
   - `Active Buyer` — currently looking
   - `Closed - [Month Year]` — last close date

### How to Tag in GHL
1. Open buyer contact
2. Go to Tags section
3. Add relevant tags
4. Save

### Why Tagging Matters
- Find the right buyers for each deal quickly
- Track buyer quality over time
- Know who to call first
- Build segments for marketing

## 5.4 Marketing Properties

### When You Get a New Deal
1. Review property details from AM
2. Prepare marketing package:
   - Address
   - Price (ask)
   - ARV
   - Repair estimate
   - Photos (if available)
   - Special notes

### How to Blast Deals
1. **Priority Buyers First** — Direct call/text
2. **Qualified Buyers** — Email/text blast
3. **Wider List** — If no bites in 24-48 hours

### Marketing Channels
- Direct text/call to top buyers
- Email blast
- Buyer Facebook groups (if applicable)
- JV partners

### Sample Deal Blast
```
🏠 NEW DEAL — [ADDRESS]

Price: $[X]
ARV: $[Y]
Repairs: $[Z]
Profit potential: $[P]

Cash or hard money. Need to move fast.
Reply YES for details.
```

## 5.8 Assignment vs Double Close

### Assignment
- Assign contract directly to buyer
- Buyer pays assignment fee at closing
- Simplest, fastest method
- Buyer sees your profit (assignment fee)

### Double Close
- You close with seller, then immediately with buyer
- Two separate transactions
- Buyer doesn't see your profit
- More complex, higher costs
- Used when spread is large or buyer sensitive

### When to Use Which
| Situation | Method |
|-----------|--------|
| Normal deal, trusted buyer | Assignment |
| Large spread ($20k+) | Consider double close |
| Buyer asks about your profit | Double close |
| Speed is critical | Assignment |

## 5.9 Closing Process

### Assignment Closing
1. Confirm buyer and seller ready
2. Send assignment agreement to buyer
3. Buyer signs, pays assignment fee
4. Title company handles everything
5. Funds distributed at closing

### Your Checklist
- [ ] Buyer has proof of funds
- [ ] Assignment agreement signed
- [ ] Title company has all docs
- [ ] Closing date confirmed
- [ ] Everyone knows where to go

### After Closing
1. Update GHL → Won
2. Log profit in KPI tracker
3. Send thank you to buyer
4. Update buyer tags (Closed - [Month])
5. Celebrate! 🎉
