# Cold Caller Quick-Start Guide
**Role:** Lead Generation (Cold Caller)
**Your Job:** Find motivated sellers from cold lists
**Day 1 Read Time:** 10 minutes

---

## Your Mission
Call through lists → Find people open to selling → Submit qualified leads to the system

You're the top of the funnel. More quality leads = more deals for the team.

---

## The Numbers
- **100+ dials/day** typical volume
- **5-10%** answer rate
- **1-3 leads/day** submitted
- **Quality > Quantity** — bad leads waste everyone's time

---

## Your Daily Flow

### Start of Shift
1. Get your call list (BatchLeads export or assigned list)
2. Open dialer
3. Have script ready

### During Calls
1. Work through list systematically
2. Mark outcomes (answered, voicemail, bad number, etc.)
3. Submit leads immediately when qualified
4. Take 5-min break every hour

### End of Shift
1. Report numbers (dials, contacts, leads submitted)
2. Note any list quality issues
3. Prep for next session

---

## The Call (30-Second Version)

```
1. "Hi, is this [Name]?"
2. "This is [You] calling about your property on [Address]..."
3. "Have you thought about selling?"
   - YES → Qualify further
   - NO → "No problem, have a good day"
4. If interested: "What would make you consider an offer?"
5. Get basic info: timeline, condition, price range
6. "I'll have someone from our team reach out to discuss further"
7. Submit lead
```

**Keep it short.** Your job is to find interest, not close deals.

---

## What Makes a Good Lead

✅ **Submit if:**
- They're open to hearing an offer
- There's some motivation (even vague)
- Property exists and they own it
- They're willing to talk more

❌ **Don't submit if:**
- Hard "no" with zero interest
- Wrong number / doesn't own property
- Hostile and won't engage
- Already listed with realtor (unless motivated)

---

## Quick Disqualify Phrases

When you hear these + no motivation → move on:
- "Not interested" (firm)
- "Take me off your list"
- "I'd never sell for less than [retail price]"
- "I love my home, never selling"

**Exception:** If they say no BUT mention a problem (tenant issues, tax trouble, inherited property) → dig deeper. Pain = potential.

---

## Submitting Leads

When you find a good one:
1. Collect: Name, phone, property address
2. Note: What they said, why they might sell
3. Submit through [your submission process]
4. Lead goes to Data Manager → LM

**Good notes matter.** LMs use your notes to prepare their call.

---

## Power Play Campaigns

High-intensity pushes on hot lists:
- Higher volume, tighter timeframe
- Team coordination
- Goal: Saturate a list quickly

When Power Play is active:
- Clear your schedule
- Max dial volume
- Fast disqualify, fast submit

## Common Objections

| They Say | You Say |
|----------|---------|
| "How'd you get my number?" | "Public records. We reach out to homeowners in the area." |
| "Is this a scam?" | "No scam — we're local investors. You can look us up." |
| "I'm not interested" | "No problem at all. Have a great day." |
| "What would you pay?" | "That depends on the property. If you're open to it, I can have someone discuss specifics." |

**Don't argue.** Friendly, professional, move on.
