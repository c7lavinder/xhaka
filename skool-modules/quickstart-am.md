# AM Quick-Start Guide
**Role:** Acquisition Manager
**Your Job:** Turn appointments into contracts
**Day 1 Read Time:** 10 minutes

---

## Your Mission
Visit properties → Evaluate deals → Make offers → Get contracts signed

You're the closer. LMs qualify and set appointments. You convert them to deals.

---

## The Numbers
- **~100%** of appointments → Offer made (every apt should end with an offer)
- **~20%** of offers → Contract signed (1 in 5, varies by lead source)
- **Goal:** 2-3 contracts/month

---

## Your Daily Flow

### Morning
1. Check today's walkthrough schedule
2. Review property details for each appointment
3. Prep materials (contract, calculator, notepad)

### At the Walkthrough
1. Build rapport (5 min)
2. Tour property, take notes/photos
3. Assess repairs mentally
4. Confirm motivation and timeline
5. Set offer call time before leaving

### After Walkthrough
1. Run numbers in MasterSuite
2. Prepare offer
3. Make offer call at scheduled time

### End of Day
1. Log offers in KPI spreadsheet (don't need to log walkthroughs)
2. Update opportunities in GHL
3. Follow up on pending offers

---

## The Walkthrough (Quick Version)

```
1. Arrive 5 min early
2. Greet warmly → "Thanks for having me out"
3. Tour the property room by room
4. Note: roof, HVAC, foundation, major repairs
5. TAKE PHOTOS — every room, exterior, problem areas
6. Ask: "Anything I should know about the property?"
7. Confirm: "When's a good time for me to call with numbers?"
8. Schedule offer call before you leave
```

⚠️ **No photos = no walkthrough.** Pictures are required for deal analysis and dispo.

**Never make an offer at the walkthrough.** Always schedule a separate call.

---

## The Offer Call (Quick Version)

```
1. Recap walkthrough → "I took a look at everything..."
2. Present the math → ARV, repairs, your offer
3. Pause. Let them respond.
4. Handle objections
5. Negotiate if room exists
6. Close → "If we can agree on $X, I can send the contract today"
```

**The pause is powerful.** State your number and wait.

---

## The Math

```
MAO = ARV × 0.70 - Repairs - Assignment Fee

Example:
ARV: $200,000
× 0.70 = $140,000
- Repairs: $30,000
- Assignment: $15,000
= MAO: $95,000
```

Know your numbers cold. Sellers respect confidence.

## Offer Outcomes

| Outcome | Next Step |
|---------|-----------|
| **Accepted** | Send contract, celebrate |
| **Counter** | Evaluate, counter back or accept |
| **Rejected** | Hot/Warm follow-up, stay in touch |
| **Ghosted** | Follow-up sequence, try 3x then cold bucket |

---

## Contract Signed → What Happens

1. Upload signed contract to GHL
2. Deal moves to "Under Contract" stage
3. Automation creates Dispo opportunity
4. Notify Esteban for buyer blast
5. Engage title company
6. You're done (unless issues arise)
